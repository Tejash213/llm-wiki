## Summary
Stemmons Enterprise is the initial commercial product name for the enterprise operations platform developed from Boxer Property's internal software.

## Key Points
- The platform was originally offered externally under the company name Stemmons with the product Stemmons Enterprise.
- Stemmons Enterprise implemented connectivity, metadata tagging, and workflow capabilities tailored to real estate operations.
- The product later rebranded as Brava Systems when enhanced AI capabilities were introduced.
