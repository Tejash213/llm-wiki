## Summary
Stemmons Enterprise was the original software product developed by [[Boxer Property]] for [[Commercial Real Estate]] operations, later rebranded as [[Brava Systems]].

## Key Points
- Developed to address operational needs in [[Commercial Real Estate]].
- Became available externally due to industry demand.
- Rebranded to [[Brava Systems]] with the introduction of enhanced [[AI Capabilities]].
