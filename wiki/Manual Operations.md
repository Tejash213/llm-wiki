## Summary
Manual operations describe traditional, dashboard-centric workflows in companies that require continuous human attention to mechanics, logistics, and decision-making.

## Key Points
- Involve tasks such as starting processes, monitoring, reacting to obstacles, and making decisions with limited information.
- Prevent focus on higher-level tasks until completion.
- Are contrasted with [[Agentic Orchestration]] in [[Self-Driving Company]]s.
