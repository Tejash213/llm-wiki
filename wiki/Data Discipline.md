## Summary
Data Discipline refers to the careful management of internal data interactions by [[AI Agents]], including detection logic, exception handling, and ensuring outputs remain internal and secure.

## Key Points
- [[AI Agents|Agents]] interact with internal data but output remains internal comments.
- Risks such as passwords in case text require precise detection logic and exception handling.
- Context-sensitive rules are implemented to avoid false positives (e.g., legitimate password resets).
