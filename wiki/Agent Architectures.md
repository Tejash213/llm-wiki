## Summary
Agent Architectures refer to the structural design of [[AI Agents]], ranging from single-shot prompts to [[Multi-Agent Workflows|Multi-Agent Systems]] with knowledge files, tool calls, grounding, memory, and workflow orchestration.

## Key Points
- Single-shot prompts are foundational but limited.
- [[Multi-Agent Workflows|Multi-agent systems]] enable compound tasks such as classification, extraction, and research.
- Agents can have [[Operational Memory]] for session coherence and longer-term memory for cross-case learning.
- Examples include loan helper, lease writing, document classifier, and strategic planner agents.
