## Summary
The [[Supply Chain Metaphor for AI]] compares the distribution and integration of [[AI Solutions Inventory]] to the logistics of consumer goods, emphasizing the importance of ecosystem and [[Orchestration Platform]].

## Key Points
- [[AI Solutions Inventory]] are likened to products distributed through a supply chain, requiring coordination among users, distributors, and manufacturers.
- The metaphor highlights the need for [[Orchestration Platform]] to manage diverse [[AI Applications]].
