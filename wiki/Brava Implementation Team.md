## Summary
The [[Brava Implementation Team]] is responsible for setting up [[Brava Systems]] instances for clients and assisting with platform [[AI Integration]] and account management.

## Key Points
- Handles initial setup of [[Brava Systems|Brava]] for clients.
- Can connect to existing AI platform accounts or manage new account setup.
- Advises client IT teams or provides managed services for account management.
- Ensures data segregation and secure billing for customers.
