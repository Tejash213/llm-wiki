## Summary
Agentic Systems are [[Agentic Framework|AI frameworks]] where multiple agents, either single or cooperating, work together to automate and optimize organizational tasks and workflows.

## Key Points
- Includes both individual agents and orchestrated [[multi-agent workflows]].
- Enables advanced [[automation]] and [[AI Integration]] across departments.
- Used in [[Boxer]]'s [[AI Solutions Inventory]] for complex tasks.
