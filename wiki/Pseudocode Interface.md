## Summary
The [[Pseudocode Interface]] is a human-readable, intermediate representation generated by [[AI Agents|agents]] to bridge intent and execution, enabling portability and reducing brittleness across schemas and systems.

## Key Points
- Acts as a durable interface between human intent and machine execution.
- Can be compiled or transpiled into SQL, Python, or other query languages.
- Standardized, versioned, and maintained for cross-system interoperability.
