## Summary
[[Internet Research Agents]] are [[AI agents]] equipped with web search capabilities to support security teams in gathering threat intelligence, subject to controlled tool access and validated sources.

## Key Points
- Support security teams by gathering threat intelligence automatically.
- Tool access and source validation are strictly controlled.
- Rate limits and privacy/compliance considerations are implemented.
