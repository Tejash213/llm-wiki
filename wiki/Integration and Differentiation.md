## Summary
Argues that the platform's unified data layer and agent integration provide competitive differentiation by enabling specialized and general AI operations.

## Key Points
- Integration of agents with a structured, unified data layer distinguishes the company's platform from competitors.
- Unified data models enable interoperability between agents and prevent isolated data silos.
- Differentiation arises from the ability to support both cross-functional and domain-specific AI use cases.
