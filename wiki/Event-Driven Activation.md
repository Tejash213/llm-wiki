## Summary
Event-Driven Activation is a method of starting [[Agentic AI|AI]] processes based on external system events, such as receiving an email or closing a [[Task]].

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] can be activated by events like email arrival, [[Task]] creation, or system alerts.
- Event-driven activation enables [[Automation]] beyond manual user initiation.
- It is useful for integrating [[AI Integration]] into business workflows.
