## Summary
The [[Property Management Expert Agent]] is a [[Domain Expert Agents]] providing specialized advice and operational support for property management tasks within a [[Self-Driving Company]].

## Key Points
- Accesses work orders, customer data, repair histories, vendors, terminology, checklists, and reports.
- Acts as a seasoned departmental veteran, offering advice and guidance.
- Integrates into general workflows when specialty knowledge is required.
