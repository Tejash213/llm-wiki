## Summary
The Property Management Expert Agent encapsulates property management knowledge—work orders, repair histories, vendors, checklists, recurring tasks, and reports—to advise and act like an experienced departmental veteran.

## Key Points
- Has access to operational records (work orders, customers, repair histories) and task/process documentation to provide contextual recommendations.
- Can be queried conversationally for advice (e.g., "What would you do here?") and integrated into broader flows when specialty knowledge is required.
- Serves both advisory and operational roles, supporting consistency and institutional memory.
