## Summary
Microsoft Foundry is a service providing video avatar technology used in the [[AI Interview Agent]], offering synthesized speech and visual interfaces.

## Key Points
- Provides video avatars for AI-driven interview platforms.
- Costs approximately $5.00 for ten minutes, considered expensive for widespread use.
- Voice-only avatars are recommended for cost management.
