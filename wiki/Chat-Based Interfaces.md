## Summary
Conversational agents that can access, query, and report on structured enterprise data through natural language interactions.

## Key Points
- Allow users to retrieve structured data like customer lists or building-specific records via conversation.
- Rely on the unified data layer for accurate, contextual responses.
- Often mediated by the AI orchestration layer to select models and manage data access.
