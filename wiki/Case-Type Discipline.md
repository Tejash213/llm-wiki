## Summary
Case-type discipline refers to the accurate classification of work cases, which is essential for operational measurement, reporting, and risk management.

## Key Points
- Misclassifying work into ad hoc case types undermines reporting, security, and operational measurement.
- Case-type fidelity is a control, metric, and risk boundary, not just a clerical task.
- Enforcement includes training, job aids, system prompts, and [[Understanding Agent|AI Classifiers]] as first-pass advisors.
- Compliance is measured and attached to reporting and security policies.
