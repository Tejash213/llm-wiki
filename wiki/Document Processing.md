## Summary
Document Processing involves the automated analysis, profiling, and summarization of documents and attachments using [[AI Applications]].

## Key Points
- Includes Document Profiler for Attachments, Attachment Short Summary, and Attachment Medium Summary.
- Used to extract and organize key information from uploaded files.
- Supports both brief and medium-length summaries.
