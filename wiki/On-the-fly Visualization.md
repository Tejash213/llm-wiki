## Summary
On-the-fly visualization refers to dynamically generated visual representations and views created at runtime to support immediate analysis and decision-making.

## Key Points
- Generated dynamically by code to produce context-appropriate views without manual dashboard construction.
- Enhances speed of insight and exploratory analysis by tailoring visual outputs to the task.
- Often integrated with "Show Me" mode to enable rapid review and human-in-the-loop decisions.
