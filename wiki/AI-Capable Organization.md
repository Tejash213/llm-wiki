## Summary
An [[AI-Capable Organization]] is structured to deliver scalable [[AI Strategy|AI Impact]] through a small, skilled team, embedded tools, and [[Event-Driven Automation]], rather than broad prompt fluency.

## Key Points
- Most employees consume well-designed [[Flexible AI Tools|AI Tools]]; a select group designs, integrates, and governs them.
- Event-driven, embedded [[AI Interventions]] are prioritized over manual prompting.
- [[Headless Architecture]] and API-first strategies enable speed, control, and reuse.
- The optimal stance is to be a "cook"—adapting and assembling solutions—rather than a "restaurant" (buying everything) or "farmer" (building everything).
