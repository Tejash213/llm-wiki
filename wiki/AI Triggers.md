## Summary
[[AI Triggers]] are mechanisms that initiate AI actions or workflows, often based on document-based events or workflow steps.

## Key Points
- Used to embed AI outputs into tasks, lists, standalone applications, and [[Multi-Agent Workflows|workflows]].
- Facilitate autonomous AI decision-making and context-aware interventions.
