## Summary
Domain expert agents are specialized [[Agentic AI]] agents embedded within workflows to provide functional intelligence and expertise at critical points in a [[Self-Driving Company]].

## Key Points
- Serve as “phone a friend” resources for [[Domain-Specific AI Agents]] knowledge.
- Examples include [[Deals Analysis Agent]] and [[Property Management Expert Agent]].
- Components may include semantic layers, glossaries, SOPs, historical data, and helper subagents.
- Enable streamlined evaluation, due diligence, and operational advice.
