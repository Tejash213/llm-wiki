## Summary
Domain Expert Agents are specialized agents that embed functional expertise (e.g., deals analysis, property management) to advise and act like seasoned departmental veterans within workflows.

## Key Points
- Provide targeted knowledge, checklists, SOPs, and historical context to inform decisions and actions.
- Integrated into general flows to "phone a friend" when specialized judgment or procedures are required.
- Examples include Deals Analysis Agent and Property Management Expert Agent with dedicated semantic layers and process documentation.
