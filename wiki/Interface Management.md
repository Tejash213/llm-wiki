## Summary
Interface management refers to the design and control of user-facing and machine-facing interfaces that present or route AI outputs and manage interactions with agents and models.

## Key Points
- While on-screen displays are useful for demos, the primary value is routing AI outputs into workflows and records rather than merely visual presentation.
- Interface management includes the user experience for tasks, links to source documents, and controls for agent configuration and oversight.
- It is coordinated by the orchestration layer to abstract complexity and present actionable items to users.
