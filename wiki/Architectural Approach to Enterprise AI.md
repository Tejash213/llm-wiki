## Summary
The [[Architectural Approach to Enterprise AI]] emphasizes building a robust foundational framework before pursuing specific [[AI Use Cases]], prioritizing data availability, [[AI Orchestration]], and defined [[AI Capabilities]].

## Key Points
- The prevailing use-case-first strategy in [[Enterprise AI Platform|Enterprise AI]] is considered flawed and leads to organizational paralysis.
- A sustainable [[Enterprise AI Rollout|AI Rollout]] requires establishing a strong architectural foundation, including corporate [[Data Infrastructure]], orchestration frameworks, and core [[AI Capabilities]].
- The recommended sequence is architecture, then capabilities, then use cases, enabling responsible innovation and organic emergence of use cases.
- This approach avoids fragmented, brittle solutions and supports consistent expansion of [[AI Capabilities]] across departments.
