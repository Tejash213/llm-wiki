## Summary
Authority Separation is the practice of maintaining a clear distinction between [[Agentic AI|AI]]-generated comments and operational actions, ensuring that [[Agentic AI|AI]] does not gain authority until reliability and policy coverage are verified.

## Key Points
- Strict separation between comment generation and operational actions is enforced.
- Authority is granted only after robust exception handling, audit trails, and confidence thresholds.
- Evidence thresholds are required to move from comment-only to limited actions.
