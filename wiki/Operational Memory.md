## Summary
[[Operational Memory]] is a feature of [[AI Agents]] that supports within-session coherence and, when used cautiously, longer-term learning across cases, with privacy and compliance considerations.

## Key Points
- Enables agents to maintain context within a session.
- Supports cross-case learning with privacy safeguards.
- Used for improving workflow sophistication and compound tasks.
