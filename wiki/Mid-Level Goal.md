## Summary
A [[Mid-Level Goal]] is a detailed objective that falls under a [[High-Level Goal]], providing clearer direction and linking to specific [[Task]]s in [[Strategic AI Planning]].

## Key Points
- Mid-level goals include accountability, benefit measurement, cost management, data and integration infrastructure, organizational approaches, workforce development, management and delivery, user enablement and access, workflows and automation, provider integration, solution development, technical capabilities, governance and oversight, security and data protection, and solution selection and management.
- Each mid-level goal is supported by specific tasks.
