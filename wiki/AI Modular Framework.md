## Summary
The [[AI Modular Framework]] describes [[AI Use Cases]] as consisting of four components: [[Action Agent|Action]], [[Data Infrastructure|Data]], [[AI Model]], and Results, each playing a distinct role in operational workflows.

## Key Points
- Every [[AI Use Cases|AI Use Case]] includes an initiating [[Action Agent|Action]], [[Data Infrastructure|Data]], a [[AI Model]] to perform the work, and Results used in various ways.
- This framework guides the integration of [[Agentic AI|AI]] into [[Commercial Real Estate]] operations.
