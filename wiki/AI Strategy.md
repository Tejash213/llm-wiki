## Summary
AI strategy refers to the intentional planning and [[AI Roadmap|roadmap]] for integrating [[Artificial Intelligence Integration|artificial intelligence]] into enterprise operations, focusing on [[Enterprise Transformation|transformation]] rather than incremental improvement.

## Key Points
- AI strategy involves transitioning from experience-based hiring to [[Aptitude-Based Hiring|aptitude-based hiring]], leveraging [[Agentic AI|AI]] for [[Knowledge Recall|knowledge recall]] and [[Pattern Recognition|pattern recognition]].
- It requires a collaborative approach, often measured by a company's [[Collaboration Quotient|collaboration quotient]] (CQ).
- Strategic [[AI Roadmap|roadmaps]] break down [[High-Level Goal|high-level goals]] into [[Mid-Level Goal|mid-level goals]] and defined [[Task|tasks]].
- [[Principled Incrementalism|Principled incrementalism]] is an alternative to full-scale transformation, focusing on incremental, principle-driven decisions.
