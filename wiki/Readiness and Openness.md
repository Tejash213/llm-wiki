## Summary
Readiness and openness describe the tendency of overseas hires and global teams to adopt AI tools enthusiastically, often amplifying the impact of training and tooling investments.

## Key Points
- Overseas hires in roles like accounting or architecture are often more willing to use AI tools than domestic counterparts.
- Training accelerates adoption and productivity improvements among global teams.
- Readiness contributes to the virtuous cycle where tooling and systems attract further global collaboration.
