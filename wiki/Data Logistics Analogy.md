## Summary
An analogy comparing data management to supply chain logistics to emphasize that moving, tracking, and delivering data reliably is as operationally critical as physical product logistics.

## Key Points
- The analogy highlights operational disciplines: staging, transport, quality control, and monitoring applied to data flows.
- Thinking of data as a logistical problem reframes investment and organizational attention toward pipelines and operational reliability.
- This perspective helps justify infrastructure and process investments needed for scalable AI capabilities.
