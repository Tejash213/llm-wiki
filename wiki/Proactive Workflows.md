## Summary
Proactive workflows are automated processes initiated by system triggers where AI enriches data and advances procedures without requiring human requests.

## Key Points
- Examples include automatic asset profiling, spam filtering of leads, document summarization, and lease expiration monitoring.
- The model emphasizes feeding outputs back into operational systems (tasks, records) rather than just generating on-screen displays.
- Proactive workflows rely on consistent metadata, agent specialization, and orchestration to scale across functions.
