## Summary
Data security is a priority in [[AI Integration]], ensuring company information is protected and [[Agentic AI|AI]] use occurs within controlled environments.

## Key Points
- Company prohibits use of public AI tools with company data (e.g., ChatGPT).
- Enterprise chat and AI platforms are used to maintain data security.
- Detailed logs and monitoring ensure [[Transparency and Control]].
