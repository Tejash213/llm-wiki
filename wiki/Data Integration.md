## Summary
[[Data Integration]] is the process of capturing, structuring, and embedding user-generated data into central organizational repositories to transform ephemeral interactions into durable assets.

## Key Points
- Ensures that [[Data Infrastructure|Data]] generated by systems is not lost but becomes part of the enterprise knowledge base.
- Reduces redundancy and maintains data integrity by using canonical sources.
- Critical for building intelligent, effective organizations.
