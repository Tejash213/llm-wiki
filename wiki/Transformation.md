## Summary
Transformation refers to the organizational shift from traditional [[AI Experience]]-based systems to AI-driven processes, impacting [[AI Hiring]], [[Job Descriptions]], and workflow automation.

## Key Points
- AI reduces reliance on human experience for knowledge recall and pattern recognition.
- Requires cultural adoption, experimentation, and incremental improvements.
- Enables new possibilities for talent utilization and workflow efficiency.
