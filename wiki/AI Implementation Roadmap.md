## Summary
An [[AI Implementation Roadmap]] is a structured plan that outlines the mission, [[High-Level Goal]], [[Mid-Level Goal]], and [[Task]]s for deploying [[Agentic AI|AI]] within an enterprise.

## Key Points
- Roadmaps evolve over time and are updated for different years and quarters.
- They break down [[Transformation]] into actionable steps and tasks.
- Used by [[Brava Systems]] and other organizations for strategic [[AI Deployment Pipeline|AI deployment]].
