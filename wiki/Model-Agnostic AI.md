## Summary
Model-agnostic AI refers to the ability to integrate and orchestrate [[AI Solutions Inventory]] from multiple providers and [[AI Model]]s, selecting the best fit per task while maintaining enterprise context and governance.

## Key Points
- Supports integration with [[Microsoft Azure AI Foundry]], Google [[Gemini]], Anthropic, [[Microsoft Foundry]], and others.
- Preserves enterprise context and governance regardless of model choice.
- Enables resilience and flexibility in AI deployment.
