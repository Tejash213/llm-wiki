## Summary
Data in Solution refers to corporate or external data integrated into [[AI Solutions Inventory]], either via live connections or static uploads, to enhance [[AI Capabilities]].

## Key Points
- Data can be connected from corporate systems (e.g., customer lists, employee records).
- Enables [[Agentic AI|AI]] to operate on relevant business information.
- Can be dynamically accessed or statically included.
