## Summary
[[AI Safeguards]] are protective measures and guardrails implemented to ensure [[Agentic AI|AI]] systems operate securely, reliably, and within organizational standards, especially during early deployment stages.

## Key Points
- Prohibit external communication until outputs meet internal standards.
- Implement exception lists and context-sensitive detectors (e.g., password phrase detection).
- Maintain immutable logs of [[Action Agent|Agent]] decisions and manager interventions.
- Enforce tone, formatting, and instruction hygiene in [[Agentic AI|AI]] outputs.
