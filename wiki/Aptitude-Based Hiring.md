## Summary
Aptitude-based hiring focuses on selecting employees for their aptitude and personality traits, rather than experience, especially as [[Agentic AI|AI]] takes over [[Knowledge Recall]] and [[Pattern Recognition]].

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] reduces the need for experience-based hiring by automating [[Knowledge Recall]] and [[Pattern Recognition]].
- Organizations shift to hiring for aptitude and traits to complement [[AI Capabilities]].
- This change is central to [[Enterprise Transformation]].
