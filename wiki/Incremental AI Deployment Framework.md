## Summary
The [[Incremental AI Deployment Framework]] advocates for a methodical, stepwise rollout of [[AI Capabilities]], starting with [[Comment-First AI Governance]] agents and gradually introducing limited automation and operational authority.

## Key Points
- Deployment begins with comment-only agents embedded in workflows.
- Authority and automation are introduced in phases, contingent on evidence and audit trails.
- Metadata enrichment and pilot notifications precede substantive tool access.
- Escalation steps are clearly defined for moving from guidance to action.
