## Summary
Boxer's philosophy on [[Agentic AI|AI]] centers on leveraging technology to automate [[Knowledge Recall]] and [[Pattern Recognition]], enabling a shift in hiring and operational practices.

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] allows Boxer to reduce reliance on experience-based hiring and focus on aptitude and personality.
- The company views [[Agentic AI|AI]] as a tool to free up [[Human Resources]] for higher-value tasks.
- Boxer prioritizes adaptability and openness to systems in its workforce.
- The philosophy includes addressing [[AI Anxiety]] and promoting positive technology adoption.
