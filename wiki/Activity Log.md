## Summary
A centralized, exhaustive record of every instance of AI usage, capturing solution, data, model, and outcomes to enable governance, ROI measurement, and risk management.

## Key Points
- Provides total visibility into AI activities across the enterprise.
- Enables leaders to measure ROI and manage operational risk by tracking usage granularity.
- Document cites usage metrics as proof points for pervasive AI adoption.
