## Summary
An [[Agent Ecosystem]] is a collection of [[AI Agents]] and workflows that operate together, often using multiple [[AI Model|models]] and providers, while maintaining enterprise context and governance.

## Key Points
- Firms compose [[AI Applications|AI services]] from multiple providers, selecting per [[Task|task]].
- [[AI Agents|Agents]] validate, classify, route, and compose sub-agents within workflows.
- Context sharing and governance are central to effective agent ecosystems.
- Supports reusable agents for profiling, classification, routing, summarization, and cost extraction.
