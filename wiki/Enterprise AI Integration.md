## Summary
Enterprise [[AI Integration]] involves embedding [[Agentic AI|artificial intelligence]] into core business processes, automating workflows, and connecting disparate data sources for enhanced operational efficiency.

## Key Points
- AI is integrated as a service, not just as a tool, within enterprise workflows.
- Integration includes connecting to systems like [[Yardi]], [[Box]], and SharePoint via APIs.
- Proper tagging and metadata application transform unstructured data into searchable knowledge bases.
- The [[Orchestration Layer]] facilitates seamless integration of [[AI Capabilities]].
