## Summary
An automated workflow pattern where an event triggers a sequence of agents to perform tasks such as profiling, extraction, summarization, and storing structured data without human intervention.

## Key Points
- Can be initiated by events like document uploads, leading to automated profiling and data extraction.
- Enables end-to-end automation where results are written back into the system's data layer.
- Often combines specialized agents and validation steps to ensure business-rule compliance.
