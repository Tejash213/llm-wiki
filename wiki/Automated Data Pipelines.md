## Summary
Automated Data Pipelines are conceptual systems that ingest unstructured data, use [[Agentic AI|AI]] to classify and summarize it, and add structured information to a searchable knowledge base.

## Key Points
- Ingests data from recordings, emails, and other sources.
- Uses [[Agentic AI|AI]] for classification and summarization.
- Adds structured data as embeddings or entities to the enterprise knowledge base.
- Forms the foundational infrastructure for [[Automated Data Pipelines]] for intelligent enterprise operations.
