## Summary
[[Results Handling]] describes how [[Agentic AI|AI]] outputs are managed, including display, automated actions, and human review.

## Key Points
- Results can be displayed on screen, sent as code to other systems, or used to trigger actions.
- Human review may be required before actions are executed.
- Flexible handling of results enables broader automation and transformation.
