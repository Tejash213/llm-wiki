## Summary
Healthy risk taking refers to fostering a culture where staff are encouraged to experiment with [[Agentic AI|AI]], learn from both successes and failures, and iteratively improve [[AI Solutions Inventory]].

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] promotes a safe environment for experimentation with [[Flexible AI Tools|AI tools]].
- Supports continuous learning and innovation.
- Enables incremental improvement and adaptability in business processes.
