## Summary
Foundry is a workflow platform used by [[Boxer]] to build and orchestrate complex [[AI Agents]] and [[AI Solutions Inventory]], including advanced functionalities and integrations.

## Key Points
- Supports the development of agents like [[Understanding Agent]], [[Insight Navigator]], [[Action Agent]], and [[Fleet Manager]].
- Enables orchestration, security, and logging for [[AI Solutions Inventory]].
- Isolates complexity and facilitates rapid deployment of AI workflows.
