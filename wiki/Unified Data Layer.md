## Summary
A consistent, centralized representation of organizational data that provides the context required by AI models and applications across the enterprise.

## Key Points
- Provides rich contextualized data that improves AI model performance.
- Acts as the feedback sink where AI outputs and structured data are stored to continuously enrich the system.
- Prevents fragmentation of the technology stack by ensuring shared data semantics.
