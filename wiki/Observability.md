## Summary
Observability in [[Agentic Systems]] refers to the centralized logging, analytics, and trace export mechanisms that enable operational oversight, audit, and performance tuning.

## Key Points
- Central logs capture agent calls, interventions, data access, and outcomes.
- Metrics include usage, cost estimates, and success/failure rates.
- Python front-end provides analytics and intervention capabilities.
