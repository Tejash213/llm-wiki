## Summary
The [[Integrated Intelligence Framework]] is an architectural approach for modern enterprises, enabling seamless [[AI Orchestration]] of data, systems, and AI to create a unified, adaptive operational environment.

## Key Points
- Replaces siloed departmental structures with a single interconnected system, akin to a central nervous system.
- Functions as smart middleware, abstracting complexity and automating processes behind the scenes.
- Provides adaptability, allowing organizations to swap [[AI Model]]s and platforms without vendor lock-in.
- Acts as a shock absorber for technological shifts, supporting [[Enterprise Transformation]].
