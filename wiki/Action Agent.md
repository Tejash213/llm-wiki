## Summary
[[Action Agent]] is an [[Agentic AI|AI]] component at [[Boxer]] capable of performing actions such as creating cases, adding documents, and changing statuses within organizational workflows.

## Key Points
- Executes actions based on interpreted data and user requests.
- Can be used individually or in combination with other [[AI Agents]] for complex workflows.
- Supports integration with chat and front-end interfaces.
