## Summary
An architectural philosophy favoring dynamic, reusable components and modular layers over rigid, pre-configured software suites to accelerate innovation and reduce balkanization of the technology stack.

## Key Points
- Encourages building platforms from reusable elements and shared core functions.
- Supports rapid innovation and bespoke solutions without deploying independent software for every need.
- Works in tandem with centralized data and AI orchestration layers to create a unified intelligent enterprise.
