## Summary
A [[Large Language Model]] (LLM) is an [[Integrated AI Systems|AI system]] trained on vast datasets to understand and generate human language.

## Key Points
- [[LLMs]] use machine learning to predict and generate text based on patterns in data.
- They are used for content creation, summarization, and real-time language understanding.
- LLMs are foundational to generative [[AI Applications]] in [[AI in Real Estate]] and other industries.
