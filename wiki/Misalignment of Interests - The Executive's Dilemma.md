## Summary
Describes tension between shareholder value and employees' personal incentives, particularly where executives without equity may prioritize job preservation over transformational change.

## Key Points
- Historically aligned incentives (employee knowledge valuable to company) are disrupted by AI making knowledge broadly available.
- Executives may implement superficial AI (performative AI) to appear innovative while avoiding projects that threaten their roles.
- This misalignment can slow necessary modernization and favor short-term appearances over long-term competitiveness.
