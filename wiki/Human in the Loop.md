## Summary
[[Human in the Loop]] refers to the principle of augmenting automated [[Multi-Agent Workflows|AI workflows]] with human oversight, allowing users to review, refine, and initiate specialized analyses as needed.

## Key Points
- [[Automation]] is balanced with opportunities for human review and deeper analysis.
- Users can trigger specialized [[AI Agents]] for tasks like due diligence or summarizing comment threads.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is context-aware and can provide tailored [[AI Recommendations]] based on user identity and task.
