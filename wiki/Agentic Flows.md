## Summary
Agentic flows describe sequences of autonomous, agent-driven actions that monitor data, perform analysis, and create tasks or updates without direct human initiation.

## Key Points
- They enable processes like monthly lease renewal analysis, external research, synthesis, and task creation assigned to responsible managers.
- Agentic flows rely on persistent context (memory) and specialized instructions to perform domain-specific duties.
- These flows transform incoming data events (e.g., document uploads, leads) into actionable operational items.
