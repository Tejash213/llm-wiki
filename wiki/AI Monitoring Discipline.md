## Summary
[[AI Monitoring Discipline]] refers to the continuous, structured review of [[Agentic AI|AI]] outputs and workflows by organizational leaders to ensure quality, detect edge cases, and refine instructions.

## Key Points
- Daily 15-minute reviews by [[Advisory Council|domain leaders]] are recommended.
- Manual crawling surfaces contextual nuance and informs better prompts and policies.
- Monitoring is ongoing and should not be “set and forget.”
- Audit logs and evidence thresholds are maintained for authority gating.
