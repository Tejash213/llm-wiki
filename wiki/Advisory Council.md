## Summary
An [[Advisory Council]] is a strategic asset composed of industry veterans, customers, and technology specialists who provide feedback and guidance on [[AI Adoption]] and product development.

## Key Points
- Offers real-world feedback on [[AI Roadmap]]s, UI design, and feature development.
- Validates strategic direction and aligns development with market needs.
- De-risks product development and accelerates commercial viability.
- Members bring diverse perspectives, including long-term industry experience and innovation expertise.
