## Summary
The document uses a driving metaphor to contrast manual, attention-intensive human workflows with a self-driving mode where systems assume planning, sensing, and execution while keeping humans in the loop for high-level input and redirection.

## Key Points
- Manual driving represents continuous human attention to mechanics, navigation, and reactive decision-making with limited scope for higher-level tasks.
- Self-driving shifts system responsibility to identity recognition, routing, autonomous planning, real-time adjustments, and proactive advisories while humans supply destinations and override when needed.
- The metaphor frames organizational transformation from human-executed tasks to systems that orchestrate multiple components and interactions.
