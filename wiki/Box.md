Box is referenced as an example where the strategy of leveraging global talent and separating knowledge work from physical work was implemented and recommended from experience.

## Key Points
- Cited as an experience-based recommendation to lean into global talent strategies.
- Example supports the principle that global teams can be combined with domestic offices for community, cybersecurity, and compliance needs.
- Demonstrates practical benefits like cost savings and improved turnaround when information work is distributed.
