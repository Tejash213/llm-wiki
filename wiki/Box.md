## Summary
[[Box]] is a company referenced in the briefing as an example of implementing [[Global Talent]] strategies and [[Agentic Orchestration]] in knowledge work.

## Key Points
- Experience-based recommendations from [[Box]] inform the [[Global Talent Strategy]].
- Box separates knowledge work from physical work and leverages annotation systems.
- The company combines domestic offices with international teams for [[AI for Operational Efficiency]].
