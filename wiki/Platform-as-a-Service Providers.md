## Summary
[[Platform-as-a-Service Providers]] (PaaS) providers offer cloud-based infrastructure and tools for deploying and managing [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI Solutions]].

## Key Points
- Customers require accounts with [[Platform-as-a-Service Providers]] to use [[Brava Systems]].
- Brava recommends [[Microsoft Azure AI Foundry]] but supports other popular providers.
- PaaS accounts allow for secure data segregation and direct billing.
