## Summary
An organizational analogy where companies evolve to operate autonomously by reducing reliance on individual employee experience and embedding knowledge into systems and data layers.

## Key Points
- Becoming a self-driving company requires shifting away from treating employees as primary repositories of institutional knowledge.
- Self-driving organizations invest in structured data, automation, and AI agents to encode knowledge and business processes.
- The transition can create friction for staff whose roles are based on experience-driven value, necessitating reskilling or role redesign.
