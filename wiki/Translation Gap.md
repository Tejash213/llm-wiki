## Summary
The translation gap is the mismatch between conversational human requests and the precise technical schemas, fields, and linkages required by systems to execute those requests.

## Key Points
- Humans ask questions in natural language (e.g., "available units") while systems require specific entity definitions and relationships.
- Closing the gap requires converting qualitative intent into structured specifications for retrieval and processing.
- The gap contributes to fragmentation, manual rework, and slower responses unless addressed by translation-capable agents.
