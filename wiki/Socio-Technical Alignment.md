## Summary
Socio-technical alignment refers to harmonizing people, processes, technology, and data to achieve effective, safe, and accountable organizational workflows.

## Key Points
- [[Human Resources]] and IT share stewardship for socio-technical alignment.
- Alignment is achieved through education, work design, policy setting, and continuous improvement.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is integrated as a tool to support, not replace, human expertise.
