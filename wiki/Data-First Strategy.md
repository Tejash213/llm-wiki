## Summary
A [[data-first strategy]] prioritizes the centralization, structuring, and governance of enterprise data as the foundation for scalable applications and [[AI Integration]].

## Key Points
- Centralizes and structures company data, tagging it and enforcing a clear [[Ontology]].
- Enables consistent business logic and [[AI Deployment Pipeline]] across the enterprise.
- Reduces data fragmentation and supports rapid, resilient innovation.
- Forms the base layer for configurable applications and [[AI Orchestration]].
