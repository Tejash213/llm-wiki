## Summary
The Central AI Connector is a core component of [[Brava Systems]], acting as a universal adapter to facilitate interactions between organizational workflows and [[AI Solutions Inventory]].

## Key Points
- Processes instructions and content from users or automated systems.
- Connects to selected [[AI Model]]s or solutions and integrates results back into workflows.
- Supports endless configuration possibilities, allowing new [[AI Solutions Inventory]] to be added without disruption.
