## Summary
A form of organizational liability where critical knowledge and processes are embedded primarily in employees' experience, posing risks as AI reduces dependence on human recall.

## Key Points
- Experience debt arises when institutional knowledge is poorly documented and resides chiefly in people's memories.
- AI and structured data reduce reliance on experience, exposing organizations that have not codified knowledge.
- Addressing experience debt requires capturing tacit knowledge in systems and refactoring processes around data and automation.
