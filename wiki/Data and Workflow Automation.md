## Summary
Data and workflow automation are foundational AI capabilities that enable systems to process information, trigger actions, and streamline organizational processes.

## Key Points
- The document lists data and workflow automation as core AI categories used for real-time processing and task execution.
- Automation of repetitive tasks frees human time for higher-level problem solving and communication.
- Effective automation requires data infrastructure and organizational alignment to realize value.
