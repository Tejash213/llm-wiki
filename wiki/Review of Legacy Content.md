## Summary
Assessment and potential rebranding of past presentations and articles to align older material with current initiatives and public-facing channels.

## Key Points
- Legacy materials covered topics like digital transformation, artificial intelligence, and being data-driven.
- Rebranding can repurpose historical thought leadership to support contemporary marketing and product narratives.
- Assigning rebranding ownership (e.g., Aldo) centralizes accountability for modernizing legacy content.
