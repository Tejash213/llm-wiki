## Summary
User-Provided Data refers to information manually entered by users into [[Headless Interface|AI interfaces]], such as prompts or instructions.

## Key Points
- Basic form of data input for [[AI Model]]s.
- Requires users to know and provide relevant data.
- Often used in simple chatbot interfaces.
