## Summary
The authoritative data store for specific kinds of enterprise data; the architecture described supports a gradual assumption of system of record responsibilities by the central platform.

## Key Points
- The central platform can integrate with external systems in a view-only mode and progressively become system of record.
- Preserves data locality where appropriate (e.g., pointering to large IoT stores rather than importing them wholesale).
- Transitioning responsibilities requires careful governance and migration planning.
