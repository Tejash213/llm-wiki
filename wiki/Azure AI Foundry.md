## Summary
[[Azure AI Foundry]] is an advanced [[Enterprise AI Platform|AI platform]] used by [[Boxer Property]] for complex activations, integrated through [[Brava Systems]] using [[Model Context Protocol]] and [[Semantic Kernel]].

## Key Points
- [[Azure AI Foundry]] is used for more complex AI activations in property operations.
- Integration is achieved via [[Model Context Protocol]] and [[Semantic Kernel]], with [[Brava Systems]] as the front-end.
- The evolving ecosystem in [[Microsoft Azure]] is seen as promising for [[Commercial Real Estate]] AI applications.
