## Summary
A strategic perspective that frames AI as a core operational service embedded within enterprise workflows rather than as discrete chat or dashboard applications.

## Key Points
- AI should be woven into organizational workflows to handle knowledge retention, pattern recognition, and rote tasks, freeing humans for higher-order work.
- Successful integration emphasizes data mastery, connectivity to systems of record, and an orchestration layer to manage complexity and direct outputs back into workflows.
- The aim is to move from human-initiated AI usage to systemic, proactive automation where events trigger agentic processes automatically.
