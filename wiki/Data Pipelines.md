## Summary
[[Data Pipelines]] are systems for moving and processing data, becoming as critical to business operations as supply chains for physical products.

## Key Points
- Data should be managed as a flowing resource, not just stored.
- Effective data pipeline management fuels [[Integrated AI Systems|AI systems]] and decision-making.
- Mastery of data logistics provides competitive advantage.
