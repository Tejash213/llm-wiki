## Summary
Data pipelines are systems and processes that move, transform, and deliver data continuously to power analytics and AI; they are framed as critical operational logistics analogous to supply chain management.

## Key Points
- Data is described as flowing rather than static; managing that flow is essential for reliable AI and decision-making systems.
- Organizations must master the logistics of moving data—collection, transformation, delivery, and monitoring—to enable scalable AI.
- Companies that build robust data pipelines will be faster to deploy AI, maintain data quality, and gain competitive advantage.
