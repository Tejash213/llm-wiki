## Summary
The [[Data Layer]] is a centralized, well-organized foundation for enterprise data, serving as the single source of truth for all systems and applications.

## Key Points
- Built on taxonomy and [[Ontology]] for structured data organization.
- Enables [[Data Integration]] and interoperability across disparate systems.
- Supports [[Agentic AI|AI]] by providing rich, contextualized data.
