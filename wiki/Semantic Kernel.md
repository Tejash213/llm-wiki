## Summary
Semantic Kernel is a technology used alongside [[Model Context Protocol]] to integrate [[AI Model]]s, supporting context-aware activations and orchestration in platforms like [[Brava Systems]].

## Key Points
- Semantic Kernel enables context-aware AI activations and orchestration.
- It is used in conjunction with [[Azure AI Foundry]] and [[Brava Systems]] for seamless user experiences.
