## Summary
The use case fallacy refers to the mistaken belief that compiling exhaustive lists of [[AI Use Cases]] is an effective strategy for enterprise [[AI Adoption]], often leading to organizational stasis.

## Key Points
- Organizations are often paralyzed by the process of listing potential [[AI Use Cases]] and forming committees.
- This approach results in fruitless endeavors and delays meaningful [[AI Adoption]].
- The fallacy is addressed by prioritizing architectural foundations and core capabilities before focusing on use cases.
