## Summary
Data readiness is the state of having organized, normalized, and accessible data within an enterprise, enabling effective [[AI Integration]] and operational excellence.

## Key Points
- Achieving data readiness is challenging due to siloed budgets and responsibilities.
- [[Boxer Property]]’s focus on data stewardship and normalization supports [[AI Adoption]].
- Emerging [[Flexible AI Tools|AI Tools]] are automating data cleanup and normalization processes.
