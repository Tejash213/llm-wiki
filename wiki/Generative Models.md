## Summary
Generative models are AI systems that create new content such as text, images, or other media; Large Language Models (LLMs) are a major category of generative models discussed in the document.

## Key Points
- Generative models can create narratives, images, and other content used in customer communications and other workflows.
- The document positions generative systems (including LLMs) alongside other AI capabilities like content understanding and predictive analytics.
- Generative models enable automation of content creation, changing how organizations allocate human work.
