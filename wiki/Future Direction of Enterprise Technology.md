## Summary
The future direction of enterprise technology emphasizes increasingly autonomous [[Agentic Systems]], dynamic data mapping, and migration toward scalable, intelligent platforms.

## Key Points
- Progression from simple prompts to collaborative [[Agentic Flows]] agent swarms.
- Aim for systems that autonomously map and integrate new data sources.
- Dynamic normalization and pointer-based architecture suggest migration to scalable platforms like [[Microsoft Azure|Cosmos DB]].
