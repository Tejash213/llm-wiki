## Summary
Traditional workflows in companies are characterized by fragmented data sources, difficult data discovery, translation gaps between conversational requests and system schemas, and time-consuming manual problem decomposition.

## Key Points
- Initiation often starts with broad, conversational questions that require precise technical translation to execute against systems.
- Data discovery complexity arises from dashboards that are hard to find or insufficient and required data spread across multiple systems.
- Translation gap: human requests do not map directly to schemas, fields, or linkages, creating friction and errors.
- Fragmentation and specificity force teams to decompose problems, define joins and filters, and locate sources manually, increasing time and error risk.
