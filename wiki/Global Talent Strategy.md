## Summary
Global talent strategy is the approach of leveraging international teams to augment and reconfigure [[Remote Knowledge Work|Knowledge Work]], serving as a catalyst for [[Self-Driving Company]] architecture.

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] augments people rather than replacing them, separating [[Remote Knowledge Work|Knowledge Work]] from physical work.
- Annotation systems clarify work and enable remote value creation.
- Offices are preferred for community, cybersecurity, and compliance, but not location-bound.
- Combines domestic offices with teams in India, Honduras, Mexico, and the Philippines.
- Benefits include financial savings, improved time utilization, and access to exceptional talent.
- Overseas hires are generally more ready to adopt [[Agentic AI|AI]], and specialized [[Global Talent Strategy|AI Talent]] is more accessible.
- [[Global Teams]] reinforce the adoption of systems, data, and tooling, creating a virtuous cycle.
