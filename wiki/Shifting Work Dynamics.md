## Summary
Describes changing assumptions about employee value, with traditional reliance on experience and recall being challenged by AI and new organizational models.

## Key Points
- Historical corporate value often derived from employees' accumulated experience and recall.
- AI and automation are reducing the necessity of human memory as a core company asset, prompting insecurity among workers whose value depends on that experience.
- Organizations must reconceive roles and value propositions as aptitude and capability become more important than tenure-based knowledge.
