## Summary
Data quality and security are critical for successful [[AI Model]] training and deployment, requiring strong [[AI Governance]], stewardship, and secure infrastructure.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] maintains hundreds of millions of data points tagged with normalized metadata for model training.
- Data quality depends on a strong data dictionary, taxonomy, and stewardship culture.
- Security requires dedicated teams, tools, and long-term discipline, with secure pipelines reused across [[AI Applications]].
