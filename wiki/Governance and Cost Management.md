## Summary
Governance and Cost Management are critical aspects of [[Intelligent Enterprise Architecture]], focusing on visibility, risk management, and efficient use of [[Agentic AI|AI]] resources.

## Key Points
- Centralized logging records all [[Agentic AI|AI]] usage, enabling ROI measurement and risk management.
- Pre-vectorized indexes and [[Agentic AI|AI]]-generated digests optimize compute and token usage.
- Solution architects must design [[Agentic Ecosystem]] agents to leverage indexed resources for cost efficiency.
