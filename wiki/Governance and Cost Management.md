## Summary
An integrated approach combining exhaustive activity logging, architectural patterns (e.g., pre-vectorized indexes), and solution-architect practice to manage AI risk and control token-based costs.

## Key Points
- Activity logs provide transparency needed for ROI analysis and risk management.
- Architectural techniques reduce token consumption and keep operational costs low.
- Requires cultural and engineering practices where architects design agents to leverage indexed resources and efficient workflows.
