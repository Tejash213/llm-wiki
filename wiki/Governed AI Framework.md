## Summary
A [[Governed AI Framework]] is a structured, secure environment for deploying context-aware [[AI Agents]] and solutions, ensuring compliance, tracking, permissions, and control.

## Key Points
- Supports connections to multiple AI Providers via universal connectors.
- Enables citizen development within secure sandboxes, subject to governance and kill switches.
- Allows flexible deployment and switching between [[AI Model]]s and platforms.
