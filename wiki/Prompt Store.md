## Summary
The [[Prompt Store]] in [[Brava Systems]] is a configurable repository for storing structured [[Prompt Store|AI instructions]], enabling rapid deployment and control of [[Action Agent|AI-driven actions]] across an organization.

## Key Points
- Stores prompts as configurable records in a dataset.
- Prompts range from simple instructions to complex workflows.
- Facilitates easy creation, modification, and deployment of [[Action Agent|AI actions]].
