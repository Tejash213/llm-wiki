## Summary
Flexible AI tools are organizational resources that enable high-value, adaptable [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]], avoiding vendor lock-in and siloed applications.

## Key Points
- Flexible tools allow organizations to build custom [[AI Solutions Inventory|solutions]] and adapt quickly.
- They bridge the gap between rigid vendor offerings and ad hoc user solutions.
- Tool-based approaches are compared to modern travel and cooking metaphors.
