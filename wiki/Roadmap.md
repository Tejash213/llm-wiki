## Summary
The roadmap refers to the planned sequence of AI adoption steps and milestones described in the presentation, including current progress and forward-looking goals for integrating AI into company operations.

## Key Points
- A roadmap helps clarify where the organization is heading with AI and aligns teams on required capabilities and milestones.
- The presentation references that Boxer is already on its roadmap while reinforcing the need to understand future steps.
- Roadmaps typically include building data foundations, implementing workflow automation, piloting AI use cases, and scaling successful models.
