## Summary
Comment-First AI Governance is a disciplined approach to [[AI Deployment Pipeline|AI deployment]] that restricts early-stage [[AI Agents|AI agents]] to generating internal guidance without operational authority, emphasizing human oversight and incremental capability-building.

## Key Points
- Early-stage [[AI Agents|AI agents]] are limited to producing comments and internal guidance, not taking actions.
- Authority is granted only after proven quality, robust exception handling, and auditability.
- Leaders are responsible for shaping AI behavior, improving instructions, and monitoring outputs.
- Manual review and daily monitoring are integral to the governance process.
