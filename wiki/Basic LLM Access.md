## Summary
Basic LLM Access involves connecting to a [[Large Language Model]] to process data and instructions, providing general [[AI Capabilities]].

## Key Points
- [[LLMs]] execute instructions and return results based on input data.
- General models may not be sufficient for specialized tasks.
- Used in simple [[AI Applications]].
