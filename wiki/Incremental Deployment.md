## Summary
[[Incremental Deployment]] is a recommended approach for introducing [[Agentic AI|AI]] tools, starting with simple use cases and gradually expanding to more complex applications.

## Key Points
- [[Brava Systems]] advocates for incremental deployment to minimize disruption and de-risk adoption.
- Initial use cases are low-impact and easy to implement.
- Allows organizations to socialize [[Agentic AI|AI]] tools and build momentum.
