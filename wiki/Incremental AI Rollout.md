## Summary
Incremental AI rollout refers to the gradual, organic integration of [[Flexible AI Tools|AI tools]] into business processes, enabling steady adoption and continuous improvement.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] implemented AI incrementally, embedding tools naturally into workflows rather than deploying [[Point Solutions]].
- This approach allows for operational flexibility, scalability, and minimizes disruption.
- Supports experimentation and learning, fostering a culture of innovation.
