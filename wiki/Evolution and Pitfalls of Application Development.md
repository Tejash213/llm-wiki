## Summary
Tracks the history of application development from expensive bespoke software to SaaS and low-code platforms, and warns of integration challenges and risks posed by AI-driven app proliferation.

## Key Points
- Early software development was costly and specialized; object orientation and networks democratized app creation.
- SaaS proliferation created many specialized applications, often resulting in feature bloat and data silos.
- Low-code/no-code and AI-driven app creation lower barriers but risk institutionalizing ungoverned, fragmented application landscapes if integration and governance are neglected.
