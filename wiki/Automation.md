## Summary
Automation in [[Brava Systems]] refers to the use of [[AI Triggers]] and workflows to streamline tasks and processes, reducing manual intervention and increasing [[AI for Operational Efficiency]].

## Key Points
- Brava supports automated AI activation through patented technology and workflow triggers.
- Automations can be applied to tasks, lists, and document-based processes.
- Enhances scalability and consistency of [[AI Interventions]] across the enterprise.
