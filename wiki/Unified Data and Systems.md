## Summary
[[Unified Data and Systems]] refer to the harmonization of enterprise data and technology infrastructure, enabling seamless access, storage, and brokering of information across the organization.

## Key Points
- Enterprise taxonomy and simple integrations consolidate data from sources like [[Yardi]] and spreadsheets.
- Unified data enables data-driven decision-making and operational excellence.
- [[Integrated Systems]] reduce repetitive tasks such as asking, answering, remembering, and reminding.
