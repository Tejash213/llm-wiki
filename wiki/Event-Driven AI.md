## Summary
[[Event-Driven AI]] refers to automations and [[AI Interventions]] triggered by data changes, inbound documents, or process milestones, minimizing reliance on manual prompts.

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is embedded in workflows and activated by events and conditions.
- Most interventions are automatic, surfacing insights and actions at the right moment.
- Outperforms manual, prompt-driven usage for consistency and scale.
