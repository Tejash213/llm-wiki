## Summary
User-Driven Activation is the manual initiation of [[Agentic AI|AI]] processes by users, typically through interfaces like chatbots or buttons.

## Key Points
- Most common form of [[Agentic AI|AI]] activation.
- Relies on user interaction to start [[Agentic AI|AI]] workflows.
- Often used in basic [[Agentic AI|AI]] interfaces.
