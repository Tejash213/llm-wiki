## Summary
Anthropic is cited as an example of a specialized AI provider that can be integrated into the platform through the AI orchestration layer.

## Key Points
- Represents specialized AI services that the orchestration layer can leverage alongside larger providers.
- Supports the architecture's vendor-agnostic approach to model selection.
- Enables organizations to choose the best-fit models for specific tasks.
