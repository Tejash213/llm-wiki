## Summary
An architectural approach that integrates AI capabilities into enterprise operations via connectivity, an orchestration layer, and specialized agents to manage information flow between users, platforms, and raw models.

## Key Points
- The architecture separates human work, platforms/agents, and raw AI models with an orchestration layer that mediates and specializes information flows.
- Core responsibilities include data connectivity, agent deployment, API integration, security, and interface management to shield users from complexity.
- It enables flexible use of diverse models and data sources while allowing outputs to be written back into operational workflows rather than simply displayed.
