## Summary
Event-driven sequences that trigger agentic processes to perform tasks such as document profiling, data extraction, transformation, and storage with minimal or no human intervention.

## Key Points
- Triggered by events like file uploads and can perform full processing pipelines automatically.
- Combine specialized agents, pre-vectorized indexes, and validation steps to optimize cost and quality.
- Integral to realizing productivity gains and scaling AI usage across an organization.
