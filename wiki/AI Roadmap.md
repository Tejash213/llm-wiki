## Summary
An [[AI Roadmap]] is a structured plan provided by [[Brava AI Solutions|Brava]] to guide organizations through the adoption and scaling of [[AI Applications|AI tools and processes]].

## Key Points
- [[Brava AI Solutions|Brava]] offers an [[AI Roadmap|AI roadmap]] with actionable steps for aligning organizational goals with [[AI Adoption|AI adoption]].
- Supports both initial rollout and long-term implementation.
- Helps organizations realize the potential of advanced [[AI Capabilities|AI tools]].
