## Summary
"Show Me" mode is a capability that produces immediate, context-specific visualizations and exploratory views to support rapid review and human decision-making.

## Key Points
- Provides task-tailored visualizations on demand to answer specific questions or explore results.
- Facilitates faster human review and re-direction of agents by surfacing relevant data and patterns.
- Depends on dynamic view generation and integrated data retrieval from Search Agents.
