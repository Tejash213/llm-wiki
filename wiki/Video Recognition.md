## Summary
Video recognition refers to computer vision techniques for analyzing video streams to detect objects, activities, and incidents; the document gives an example of detecting theft.

## Key Points
- Video recognition is presented as part of vision intelligence that can detect events and provide situational awareness.
- It has applications in safety, monitoring, and operational responses within real estate assets.
- Integration with workflow automation can convert detections into alerts and actions.
