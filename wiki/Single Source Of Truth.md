## Summary
A centralized, well-organized taxonomy and ontology that serves as the authoritative data layer enabling consistent understanding and access to data across systems.

## Key Points
- Provides a consistent reference point for disparate systems to ensure data is understood and accessed uniformly.
- Underpins higher-level platform capabilities such as AI orchestration and low-code application assembly.
- Enables migration strategies where the centralized layer can gradually assume the role of the system of record.
