## Summary
Technology adoption often triggers exaggerated fears about societal disruption, but historical patterns show these anxieties rarely play out as predicted.

## Key Points
- Past predictions about technological threats (e.g., overpopulation, peak oil) were often exaggerated.
- Doomsday narratives are driven by fear of the unknown.
- Balanced perspective is important when evaluating new technologies like [[Agentic AI|AI]].
