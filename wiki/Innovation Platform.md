## Summary
An [[Innovation Platform]] is a modular system that enables rapid experimentation, deployment, and management of [[AI Agents]] tools across varied business functions.

- Facilitates rapid experimentation and contextualization for diverse business needs.
- Supports scalable and flexible [[AI Adoption]].
