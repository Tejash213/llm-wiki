## Summary
Schedule-Driven Activation refers to starting [[Agentic Orchestration|AI processes]] at regular intervals or specific times, often managed by scheduling systems.

## Key Points
- [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]] can be activated on a schedule, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
- Requires integration with scheduling systems.
- Used for routine or recurring AI tasks.
