## Summary
A variance extractor is an example of a specialized agent built to identify and act on variances (e.g., exceeding thresholds) by generating tasks and routing them to responsible parties.

## Key Points
- It is configured with business rules (e.g., identify variances >10%, compare to benchmarks) and can automatically create follow-up tasks.
- The agentic approach allows proactive remediation by assigning tasks to property managers based on detected variances.
- This demonstrates the value of specialization over generic conversational interfaces for repeatable operational processes.
