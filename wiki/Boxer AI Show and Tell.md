## Summary
An internal presentation led by Justin Segal at Boxer covering AI strategy, philosophy, vocabulary, use cases, required organizational capabilities, and a roadmap for AI adoption in real estate.

## Key Points
- The session aimed to update Boxer staff on AI initiatives, philosophy, and the company's direction with AI.
- Topics included types of AI (data/workflow automation, content understanding, vision and speech intelligence, analytical/predictive ML, generative models/LLMs), hiring and cultural implications, and roadmap considerations.
- The presentation used an external industry talk as a basis while adapting points for Boxer's internal context and strengths.
