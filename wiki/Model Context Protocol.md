## Summary
[[Model Context Protocol]] is a method used to integrate [[AI Model]]s, such as those in [[Azure AI Foundry]], into operational workflows, ensuring context-aware activations and results.

## Key Points
- [[Model Context Protocol]] is used for seamless integration of [[AI Model]]s with operational data.
- It is part of the technology stack enabling advanced AI activations at Boxer Property.
