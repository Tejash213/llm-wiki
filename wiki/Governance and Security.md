## Summary
[[Governance and Security]] are critical to enterprise [[Agentic AI|AI]] and application deployment, ensuring compliance, auditability, and secure operation across departments and systems.

## Key Points
- Enforces compliance, maintains metadata and lineage, and operates within secure environments.
- Role-based access and audit trails are essential features.
- Governance is central to integrating [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]] and maintaining enterprise context.
