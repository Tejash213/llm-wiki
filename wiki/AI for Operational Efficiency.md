## Summary
[[Agentic AI|AI]] is increasingly used in [[Commercial Real Estate]] to streamline operations, reduce repetitive tasks, and enhance employee focus on creative and interpersonal work.

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] enables operational efficiency by automating routine tasks and freeing employees for higher-value activities.
- Integration of [[Agentic AI|AI]] in daily workflows is seen as essential for competitiveness in [[Commercial Real Estate]].
- [[AI Adoption]] has led to significant increases in process activations and throughput across departments.
- The balance between [[Automation]] and human judgment remains important, especially in tenant relations.
- Incremental, configurable [[AI Deployment Pipeline]] across portfolios is preferred over single-use solutions.
