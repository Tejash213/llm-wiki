## Summary
[[Multiple Agents]] refers to the use of several [[AI Agents]] working together in orchestrated workflows, often managed by an [[Orchestration Agents]] agent.

## Key Points
- Agents collaborate to execute complex tasks and workflows.
- Orchestrator agent coordinates sub-agents and returns results.
- Agents may use different [[AI Model]]s (LLM, vision, machine learning).
