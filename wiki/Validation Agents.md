## Summary
Specialized agents deployed within workflows to ensure outputs conform to business logic and validation rules before data is accepted or propagated.

## Key Points
- Deployed modularly within workflows to validate outputs from processing agents.
- Help ensure data quality and adherence to organizational rules during automated processes.
- Provide a governance touchpoint within otherwise autonomous flows.
