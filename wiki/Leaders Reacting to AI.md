## Summary
Different leadership responses to AI range from alarmist public commentary to quieter, practical planning within organizations; the loudest public voices are often outliers compared to the steady work of many business leaders.

## Key Points
- Public discourse is dominated by a few loud voices, which can distort perceptions of how most leaders are responding to AI.
- Many companies are engaging in pragmatic questions: talent needs, where to start, and how to operationalize AI efforts.
- Effective leadership balances attention to headlines with internal planning and prioritization of business-relevant AI initiatives.
