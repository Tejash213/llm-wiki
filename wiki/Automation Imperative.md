## Summary
The [[Automation Imperative]] is the drive to shift from reactive, human-initiated [[AI Use Cases]] to systemic, automated integration of [[Agentic AI|AI]] into operational workflows for proactive business management.

## Key Points
- [[AI Agents]] proactively analyze incoming data, enrich it, and initiate processes automatically.
- Automation transforms simple data entry events into rich, decision-support-ready records.
- Over 1.5 million [[AI Use Cases]] have been recorded in one organization as of May 2026.
