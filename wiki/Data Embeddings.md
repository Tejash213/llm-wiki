## Summary
Data Embeddings are proprietary representations of organizational data used in [[AI Model]]s, crucial for maintaining control and flexibility in enterprise [[AI Strategy|AI strategies]].

## Key Points
- Should be managed internally rather than generated by third-party platforms.
- Provide operational flexibility and protect intellectual property.
- Allow organizations to supply embeddings to any [[Enterprise AI Platform|AI Platform]] or [[AI Model]].
