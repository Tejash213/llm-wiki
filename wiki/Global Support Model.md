## Summary
The [[Global Support Model]] involves establishing remote offices in multiple countries to perform [[Remote Knowledge Work]], providing cost efficiencies and access to diverse [[global talent]] pools.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] set up remote offices in India, Honduras, Mexico, and the Philippines.
- Enables 24/7 operations and reduces costs by leveraging [[global talent]].
- Supports dissection of work to determine which tasks can be done remotely or off-hours.
