## Summary
[[AI Adoption]] describes the rapid and scalable uptake of [[Agentic AI|artificial intelligence]] technologies across departments, driven by business users and continuous experimentation.

## Key Points
- Usage grew from 524 activations in September to nearly 100,000 in July.
- Adoption is unprecedented in the industry and spans many departments.
- Business users identify use cases and drive [[AI Integration]].
- Learning from pilots and feedback supports safe, scalable growth.
