## Summary
Enterprise AI rollout is the strategic process of implementing [[Artificial Intelligence Integration]] across an organization, emphasizing [[Enterprise Architecture]], [[AI Governance]], capabilities, and automated activation for sustainable adoption.

## Key Points
- Successful [[Enterprise AI Rollout|AI rollout]] requires a deliberate sequence: [[Enterprise Architecture]], [[AI Capabilities]], then [[AI Use Cases]].
- [[AI Governance]] and foundational [[AI Capabilities]] are critical to avoid fragmented, unsustainable solutions.
- Automated [[AI Triggers]] and systemic deployment maximize organizational benefits.
