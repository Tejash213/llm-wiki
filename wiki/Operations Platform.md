## Summary
An [[Operations Platform]] is a configurable system for managing business processes, data, and applications, exemplified by [[STEMIs Enterprise]] and [[Brava Systems]].

## Key Points
- Provides tools for building, maintaining, and overseeing enterprise operations.
- Supports [[Data Normalization]] and centralized repositories.
- Enables creation of interoperable, configurable applications.
