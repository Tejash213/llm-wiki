## Summary
Action and Execution refers to moving beyond visualization to trigger workflows and operational changes through human-in-the-loop decisions or agent-driven operations.

## Key Points
- The objective is operational impact: insights should lead to actions and workflow triggers.
- Supports multiple modes: human-in-the-loop where humans decide and agent-in-the-loop where specialized agents perform tasks.
- Requires integration with systems, devices, and signals to carry out tasks while preserving human expression and decision authority.
