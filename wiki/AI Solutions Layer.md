## Summary
The [[AI Solutions Layer]] is a user-friendly environment within enterprise [[AI Strategy]] architectures where power users configure specialized [[AI Applications]] built upon underlying [[Agentic Systems]].

## Key Points
- Enables customization and specialization of general agents for domain-specific tasks.
- Accessible to users with domain knowledge but not necessarily deep technical skills.
- Built atop agents developed in lower architectural layers.
