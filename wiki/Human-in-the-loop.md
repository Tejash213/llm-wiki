## Summary
Human-in-the-loop denotes workflows where humans review, decide, and authorize actions based on agent-generated outputs, maintaining final decision authority.

## Key Points
- Humans interpret visuals and insights and make decisions about next steps or approvals.
- Preserves accountability and judgment in processes that benefit from human context and nuance.
- Often used in combination with agent-in-the-loop modes for hybrid operational models.
