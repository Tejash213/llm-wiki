## Summary
Citizen-led AI development describes a decentralized approach where individuals build bespoke [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI Solutions]], often leading to fragmented and fragile ecosystems within organizations.

## Key Points
- Citizen-led development is a common overcorrection to organizational stasis in [[AI Adoption]].
- Solutions built by individuals are often dependent on single persons and become obsolete when those individuals leave.
- This approach results in brittle constructs that require replacement and undermines sustainable [[AI Adoption]].
