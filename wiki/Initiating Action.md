## Summary
[[Initiating Action]] refers to the methods by which [[Agentic AI|AI]] processes are started, including [[User-Driven Activation]], [[Event-Driven Activation]], and [[Schedule-Driven Activation]].

## Key Points
- [[User-Driven Activation]] involves manual initiation, such as clicking a button or opening a chatbot.
- [[Event-Driven Activation]] triggers [[Agentic AI|AI]] based on external events (e.g., email arrival, task creation).
- [[Schedule-Driven Activation]] starts [[Agentic AI|AI]] processes based on a predefined schedule.
