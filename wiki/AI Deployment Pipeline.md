## Summary
The [[AI Deployment Pipeline]] is an infrastructure that enables rapid creation and automated deployment of [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]] within designated organizational contexts, supporting both single-shot prompts and [[Multi-Agent Workflows|multi-agent workflows]].

## Key Points
- [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]] can be created and deployed in about five minutes.
- Supports single-shot prompts and [[Multi-Agent AI Orchestration|multi-agent systems]] with knowledge sources, tool calls, and workflow orchestration.
- [[AI Agents|Agents]] are initially comment-only, confined to specific tasks and contexts.
- Integration with internal data and [[Automation]] is configurable.
