## Summary
Remote knowledge work involves performing tasks that do not require physical presence from distributed locations, leveraging [[Global Talent]] and extended operational hours.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] dissected tasks to identify those suitable for remote execution.
- Remote offices handle knowledge work such as processing [[Invoices]], scheduling, and report generation.
- Supports cost efficiency and 24-hour coverage.
