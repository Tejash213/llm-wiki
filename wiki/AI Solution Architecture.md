## Summary
[[AI Solution Architecture]] involves designing and integrating [[Integrated AI Systems|AI systems]] to assist with [[Job Descriptions]], [[Standards]], [[CAS Jobs]], and [[Task|task]] management within an organization.

## Key Points
- Requires experimentation, incremental improvements, and cultural adoption.
- Architects design systems to support high aptitude hires.
- Integration enables [[Automation|automation]] and streamlining of workflows.
