## Summary
Computer vision is identified as one of several AI capabilities that can be integrated into enterprise workflows alongside language models and predictive analytics.

## Key Points
- Computer vision complements text-based understanding by providing image and video analysis for operational workflows (e.g., construction monitoring, asset inspection).
- Integration into the orchestration layer allows visual data to trigger agentic workflows and updates to records.
- Its effectiveness relies on connectivity to repositories and consistent metadata to contextualize visual outputs.
