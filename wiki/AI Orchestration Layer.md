## Summary
An abstraction layer that manages connections to multiple AI providers, handles model execution, data access, and orchestrates end-to-end AI-driven workflows across the enterprise.

## Key Points
- Functions as a model- and platform-agnostic intermediary that can route tasks to the most appropriate AI provider.
- Manages the AI lifecycle from prompt to data retrieval, model execution, and application of outputs.
- Enables chat-based interfaces, automated agentic workflows, and embedded AI triggers across applications.
