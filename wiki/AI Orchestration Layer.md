## Summary
The [[AI Orchestration Layer]] is a platform-agnostic intermediary that connects to multiple [[Agentic AI|AI]] providers, abstracting complexity and managing the [[AI Deployment Pipeline|AI lifecycle]] within the enterprise.

## Key Points
- Enables integration with diverse [[Agentic AI|AI]] services ([[AWS]], [[Azure AI Foundry]], [[Gemini]], Anthropic, etc.).
- Manages prompts, [[Data Access]], [[AI Model]] execution, and application output.
- Supports chat-based interfaces, automated workflows, and embedded intelligence.
