## Summary
[[Data Management Roles]] involve individuals responsible for identifying, organizing, and preparing data necessary for [[AI Capabilities]] within [[Brava Systems]].

## Key Points
- Organizations benefit from having a dedicated person focused on [[Data Management Roles]].
- Role includes identifying relevant data like customer lists, inventory, personnel, and project information.
- Can be filled by [[Brava Systems|Brava]], a third party, or an internal employee.
- Training in [[Azure AI Foundry]] is recommended.
