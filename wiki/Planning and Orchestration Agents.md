## Summary
Agents designed to manage multi-step workflows by creating and executing structured plans across tasks and teams.

## Key Points
- Coordinate complex, multi-step processes (e.g., rolling up to-do lists across large organizations).
- Generate, sequence, and monitor sub-tasks and interactions between specialized agents.
- Essential for scaling automation and ensuring consistent execution of business processes.
