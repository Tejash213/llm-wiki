## Summary
An [[Integrated Global Team]] refers to the company's highly coordinated and unified workforce across locations, enabling efficient operations and rapid innovation.

## Key Points
- Integration across teams is unparalleled in the industry.
- Supports strong data and systems foundations for [[Artificial Intelligence Integration]].
- Facilitates rapid adoption and experimentation with [[AI Adoption]] technologies.
