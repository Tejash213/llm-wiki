## Summary
Global Footprint refers to a distributed staffing model combining domestic offices with teams located in countries like India, Honduras, Mexico, and the Philippines to optimize cost, time, and talent access.

## Key Points
- Financial benefits: lower labor rates create capital for tooling and growth.
- Time utilization: extended operational hours across time zones improve turnaround and cycle times.
- Talent access: expands the pool of qualified professionals beyond domestic location constraints.
