## Summary
Content understanding covers AI capabilities that parse and interpret unstructured documents and media to produce structured insights for workflows.

## Key Points
- It involves tagging, classification, extraction, and summarization of unstructured content stored in systems like Box and SharePoint.
- The value of content understanding increases when metadata from core systems (e.g., property codes) is applied consistently.
- Outputs are routed to specialized agents or written into structured datasets for subsequent automation.
