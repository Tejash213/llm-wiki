## Summary
The virtuous cycle describes how global teams drive improvements in systems, data, and tooling, which in turn enable and strengthen global collaboration and further system adoption.

## Key Points
- Global teams encourage investment in systems and data to support distributed work.
- Improved systems and tooling make it easier to scale global collaboration and integrate specialized roles.
- Each improvement reinforces the other, creating momentum toward a Self-Driving Company model.
