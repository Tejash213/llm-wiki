## Summary
Company culture in the document emphasizes adaptability, openness to systems, and hiring people who are flexible and can work with AI and process changes.

## Key Points
- Boxer reports a culture that has spent decades hiring adaptable employees comfortable with systems and process change.
- Cultural readiness is cited as a major factor reducing anxiety and friction during AI adoption.
- Culture influences hiring priorities (aptitude over long-tenured experience) and supports organizational transformations enabled by AI.
