## Summary
Point solutions are standalone SaaS tools with limited functions and datasets, increasingly viewed as less relevant in enterprises adopting integrated data and [[AI Orchestration]] platforms.

## Key Points
- Point solutions lead to data fragmentation and duplicative logic.
- [[AI Orchestration]] and centralized data diminish the need for fragmented SaaS tools.
- The market is shifting from siloed SaaS to integrated platforms.
