## Summary
Fragmentation and specificity refer to the need to break problems into precise data components, relationships, filters, and joins, which increases effort and error in traditional workflows.

## Key Points
- Teams must decompose problems and define exact relationships, filters, and data sources, which is time-consuming.
- High specificity is needed to connect disparate datasets (e.g., linking asset managers to properties and available spaces).
- This process amplifies the value of agents that can automate decomposition and data linkage.
