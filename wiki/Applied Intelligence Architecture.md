## Summary
Applied Intelligence Architecture refers to the integration of [[Agentic AI|AI]] as a fundamental service within enterprise workflows, enabling [[Automation]], knowledge retention, and [[Pattern Recognition]].

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is woven into enterprise operations beyond conversational interfaces and dashboards.
- The architecture emphasizes [[Agentic AI|AI]] handling rote tasks, freeing human capital for creative and higher-order functions.
- [[Data Mastery]] and connectivity to core systems are central to operational excellence.
- The [[Orchestration Layer]] manages integration of diverse [[AI Capabilities]], including workflow [[Automation]] and [[Agentic Flows]].
