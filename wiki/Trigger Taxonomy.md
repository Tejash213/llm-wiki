## Summary
[[Trigger Taxonomy]] categorizes the types of actions that initiate [[Multi-Agent Workflows|AI workflows]], including human-initiated, event-driven, and workflow-based triggers.

## Key Points
- Human-initiated triggers (AI button), event-driven triggers (file attachment), and workflow triggers (approval or status change).
- Enables flexible deployment of [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]] across enterprise processes.
- Supports auditability and context sharing.
