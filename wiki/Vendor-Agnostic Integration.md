## Summary
An architectural stance in which the platform can connect to multiple AI and cloud providers, allowing selection of the most appropriate model or service per task and avoiding vendor lock-in.

## Key Points
- The AI orchestration layer abstracts provider details and routes tasks to chosen models.
- Enables flexibility to select best-of-breed services like AWS, Microsoft, Google, and specialized providers.
- Supports resilience and strategic procurement choices for enterprises.
