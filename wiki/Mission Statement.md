## Summary
A mission statement is a long-term, overarching purpose or reason for an organization, department, initiative, or business function, guiding all related [[Strategic AI Planning]].

## Key Points
- The mission rarely changes and serves as a foundation for setting goals and [[Task]]s.
- In the [[Brava Systems|Brava]] strategic plan, the mission is to leverage advanced technologies to improve enterprise-wide outcomes responsibly and flexibly.
