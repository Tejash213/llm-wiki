## Summary
The Best Wiki Skill is the optimized version of the Wiki Generation Skill produced by the SkillOpt training loop. It represents the highest-performing prompt for generating structured FutureSense wiki pages.

## Key Points
- Generated by SkillOpt iterative training on real wiki generation tasks.
- Improves on the initial_skill.md seed prompt through multiple training epochs.
- Used by the pipeline when generating wiki pages with --generate-only --skill.
- Evaluated against a validation set of FutureSense source documents.
- Encodes best practices for factual, comprehensive, and well-linked wiki pages.
