## Summary
[[Data Harmonization]] is the process of ingesting, validating, transforming, and improving data from disparate sources, enabling unified and actionable enterprise insights.

## Key Points
- Modern [[Data Pipelines]] and [[Data Embeddings|semantic layers]] allow real-time harmonization without manual [[Data Normalization]].
- [[AI Deployment Pipeline|AI-augmented pipelines]] turn legacy system data into valuable resources.
- Enables correlation of data across the enterprise for strategic decision-making.
