## Summary
Transparency in [[Agentic Systems]] involves exposing agent configurations, tool digests, and system prompts to facilitate collaborative tuning, validation, and auditability.

## Key Points
- [[Understanding Agent]] produces configuration digests for review.
- Centralized logs and exportable traces support audit and performance tuning.
- Promotes inspectable-by-design principles.
