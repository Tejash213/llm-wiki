## Summary
Predictive maintenance involves using [[Agentic AI|AI]] to anticipate equipment failures and optimize maintenance schedules, though preventative maintenance remains effective when applied with discipline.

## Key Points
- Predictive maintenance is often overvalued compared to basic preventative maintenance.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is more valuable for [[AI for Operational Efficiency]] by eliminating routine tasks and improving workflow.
