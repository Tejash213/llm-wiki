## Summary
The [[Symbiotic Enterprise]] is a strategic philosophy emphasizing the integration of [[Agentic AI|AI]], data, systems, and human workflows to create scalable, durable organizational capabilities.

## Key Points
- Advocates for seamless integration of technology into a broader ecosystem rather than standalone [[AI Applications]].
- Data generated by any system should be captured, structured, and integrated into central repositories.
- Technology implementation must consider economic viability, future compatibility, and abstraction for reusability.
- Success is measured by the degree of seamless integration into daily organizational work.
