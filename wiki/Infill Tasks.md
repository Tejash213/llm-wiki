## Summary
Infill tasks are activities performed when time permits, such as benchmarking against industry best practices, and may be assisted by [[Agentic AI|AI]].

## Key Points
- Listed in [[Job Descriptions]] as ad hoc tasks.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] can help organize and interpret complex infill tasks.
