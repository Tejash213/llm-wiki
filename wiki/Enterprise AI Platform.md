## Summary
The [[Enterprise AI Platform]] is a flexible, company-wide system that orchestrates AI actions, data, models, and results, supporting automated workflows and advanced prompts.

## Key Points
- The platform manages AI components: action, data, model, and results.
- It connects company data with AI tools from [[Microsoft Azure|Azure]], [[AWS]], and [[Gemini]].
- Orchestration enables prompts, data retrieval, model execution, and results handling.
- Supports both user-activated and automated workflows.
