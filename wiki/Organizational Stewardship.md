## Summary
[[Organizational Stewardship]] is the principle that department heads and case owners are responsible for monitoring, calibrating, and refining [[Agentic AI|AI]] behavior within their domains, ensuring accountability and fit-for-purpose deployment.

## Key Points
- Department heads own [[Agentic AI|AI]] in their domains and monitor daily.
- They gather edge cases, talk to case owners, and calibrate [[AI Agents]] behavior.
- Ownership and interfaces are assigned for refining prompts, taxonomy, and thresholds.
- Collaboration is required to standardize learnings and instruction changes across case types.
