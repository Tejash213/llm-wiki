## Summary
Rubric-Based Assessment is a proposed framework for [[AI Interview Agent]] to evaluate interview answers using structured criteria for technical and behavioral questions.

## Key Points
- Technical answers are verified against a knowledge base for accuracy.
- Behavioral answers are scored on relevance, specificity, and positivity.
- Enhances the reliability and fairness of AI-driven evaluations.
