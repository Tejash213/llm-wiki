## Summary
The SEAL team is a compact, cross-functional group within an organization responsible for designing, integrating, and governing [[AI Solutions Layer]] and automation.

## Key Points
- Composed of [[AI Solution Architecture]] architects, process engineers, agent builders, and data integration specialists.
- Focused on building patterns, integrations, and safe, sustainable automation.
- Keeps the group lean and avoids broad, organization-wide prompt fluency.
- Central to establishing [[Headless Architecture]] patterns and API-first integrations.
