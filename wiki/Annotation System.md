## Summary
An annotation system captures and describes knowledge work in structured form so that information-centric tasks can be executed by distributed teams and agents with clear instructions and expectations.

## Key Points
- Enables separation of knowledge work from physical location by documenting tasks, inputs, and expected outputs.
- Facilitates training, QA, and tooling investments by making work explicit and repeatable.
- Serves as a foundation for scaling work to global teams and integrating with agentic systems.
