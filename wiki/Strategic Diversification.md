## Summary
Strategic diversification refers to expanding a company’s portfolio into varied ventures that share operational and strategic commonalities, aiming to leverage synergies.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] is expanding beyond [[Commercial Real Estate]] into other asset classes and ventures.
- Diversification is supported by shared operational models and [[Enterprise AI Integration|AI-enabled scalability]].
- Aims to leverage synergies across different business functions.
