## Summary
A governance balance that enables employee innovation with AI while maintaining security, compliance, and operational coherence through structured frameworks and boundaries.

## Key Points
- Unrestricted access to powerful tools can lead to shadow IT, security risks, and incoherent systems.
- The recommended approach is to permit innovation within defined guardrails—teams can experiment but operate within controlled parameters.
- Frameworks should enable creativity while ensuring scalability, security, and the integrity of enterprise systems.
