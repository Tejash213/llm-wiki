## Summary
Balancing employee creativity with organizational control is crucial for managing [[Flexible AI Tools]], preventing chaos while fostering innovation within structured frameworks.

## Key Points
- Unrestricted access to [[Flexible AI Tools]] can lead to security risks and shadow IT.
- Frameworks should allow innovation within defined boundaries.
- Structured freedom enables secure, scalable, and cohesive team creativity.
