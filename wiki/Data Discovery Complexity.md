## Summary
Data discovery complexity describes the difficulty of locating, accessing, and integrating necessary data across multiple systems and formats to answer business questions.

## Key Points
- Dashboards and tools may be hard to remember, locate, or insufficient for current needs, hindering access to answers.
- Required data often spans multiple systems (e.g., rent rolls, CRM, asset manager datasets), requiring integration and schema linking.
- Complexity increases time-to-insight and causes fragmentation, manual joins, and potential errors in analysis.
