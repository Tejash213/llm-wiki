## Summary
Coding agents are [[AI Agents]] systems that leverage [[Data Embeddings|LLM-context layers]] to index repositories, documentation, and APIs, enabling structured [[Agentic AI|Agentic development]] and adaptation.

## Key Points
- Reduce the cost and time of adaptation for enterprise workflows.
- Provide a structured knowledge layer for [[Agentic AI|Agentic development]].
- Work in conjunction with [[LLM Wikis]] and [[Headless Architecture|headless architectures]].
