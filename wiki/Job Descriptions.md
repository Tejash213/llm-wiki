## Summary
Job descriptions outline the responsibilities, tasks, and requirements for specific roles within an organization, including [[Scheduled Tasks]], [[Event-Driven Tasks]], and [[Infill Tasks]].

## Key Points
- Include overview, organizational impact, systems, inputs, and outputs.
- Specify templates, instructions, and frequency for tasks.
- Event-driven and infill tasks may be assisted by [[Agentic AI|AI]].
- Can be compared and analyzed using [[Agentic AI|AI]] to identify overlaps and gaps.
