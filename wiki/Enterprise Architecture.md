## Summary
Enterprise Architecture refers to the structured design and orchestration of [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]] and workflows within [[Boxer]] to ensure coherence, scalability, and governance.

## Key Points
- Prevents chaotic, ungovernable, and un-auditable [[AI Implementation Roadmap|AI implementations]].
- Focuses on architecture, design, cohesion, and orchestration of [[AI Solutions Inventory|AI solutions]].
- Supports department heads and key individuals in process redesign and rollout.
