## Summary
The property manager is used in the document as an example role that traditionally depends on experience and pattern recognition to assess property conditions and make judgments.

## Key Points
- Property managers rely on knowledge recall and pattern recognition (e.g., assessing curb appeal or landscaping issues).
- AI can augment property managers by providing data-driven insights and pattern detection to support decision-making.
- The role illustrates how AI shifts the value proposition from pure experience toward supporting human judgment with automated insights.
