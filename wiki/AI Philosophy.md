## Summary
The document outlines a philosophical approach to AI adoption emphasizing rebalancing hiring priorities, leveraging AI for knowledge and pattern recognition, and focusing human hires on aptitude, communication, and problem solving.

## Key Points
- AI can handle substantial portions of knowledge recall and pattern recognition that companies historically paid humans to perform.
- With AI reducing the cost of those functions, organizations should hire more for aptitude, flexibility, communication, and problem solving rather than only experience.
- AI adoption should be paired with data, workflow systems, and cultural readiness to realize benefits and minimize disruption.
