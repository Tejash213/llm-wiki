## Summary
A [[Task]] is the most specific action item or work unit that directly supports a [[Mid-Level Goal]], often assigned to individuals and tracked for progress.

## Key Points
- Tasks include actions such as assigning ownership, implementing QA, flagging issues, establishing metrics, surveying users, attributing costs, and creating budget capacity.
- [[Brava Systems]] provides tools to support task completion, including templates and case management.
