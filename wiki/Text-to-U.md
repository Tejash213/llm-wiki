## Summary
[[Text-to-U]] is an [[AI Capabilities|AI Capability]] that generates HTML and data visualizations from natural language instructions, creating dynamic user interfaces on-the-fly.

## Key Points
- Text-to-U enables real-time creation of interfaces and visualizations from text, often generated by AI.
- This technology enhances [[AI Experience]] and workflow efficiency in [[Commercial Real Estate]] operations.
