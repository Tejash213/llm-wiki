## Summary
Knowledge recall is the ability to retrieve and apply relevant information, codes, meanings, and processes, either by humans or [[Integrated AI Systems|AI Systems]].

## Key Points
- Historically relied upon in [[AI Hiring]] and job performance.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] can store and instantly recall large volumes of knowledge.
- Reduces reliance on human experience for knowledge recall tasks.
