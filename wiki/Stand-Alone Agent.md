## Summary
A [[Stand-Alone Agent]] is a specialized [[Agentic AI|AI]] entity created on platforms like [[Microsoft Azure]], with custom logic, instructions, and data connections.

## Key Points
- [[AI Agents|Agents]] are instantiated and configured with specific instructions and data sources.
- Built on platforms such as [[Microsoft Azure]].
- Can be deployed and used outside the development platform.
