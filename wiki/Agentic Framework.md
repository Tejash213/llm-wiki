## Summary
The [[Agentic Framework]] describes business operations as a series of autonomous workflows executed by specialized [[AI Agents]], enabling proactive management and automation.

## Key Points
- Specialized agents perform defined processes, such as variance extraction or lease renewal analysis.
- Agents can retain context, execute complex workflows, and generate [[Task]]s automatically.
- The framework enables proactive, repeatable management across domains like service contracts and warranty expirations.
