## Summary
The agentic framework envisions business processes as autonomous agent workflows that act on behalf of users to monitor, analyze, synthesize, and create operational tasks.

## Key Points
- It operationalizes repeatable templates (e.g., lease renewals, warranty tracking) into automated agentic sequences running on schedules or triggers.
- Agents integrate external research, internal documents, and metadata to produce task-ready outputs with direct links to source clauses or evidence.
- The framework supports scalability by reusing patterns across domains using a central platform for deployment and management.
