## Summary
LLM stands for Large Language Model, a type of generative model trained on large corpora of text data that predicts sequences of words and can generate or transform text.

## Key Points
- The document references LLMs as generative AI models that underpin content creation and advanced text understanding.
- LLMs operate by predicting the next token in text sequences based on large-scale training data and model architectures.
- LLMs are listed alongside other AI categories used in real estate workflows, such as content understanding and automation.
