## Summary
[[Governance and Containment]] involve centralized deployment, sandboxing, and risk mitigation to ensure safe experimentation and stable mission-critical systems in [[AI-Capable Organization]]s.

## Key Points
- Centralized deployment pipelines and sandboxes allow creativity within controlled environments.
- SOX-compliant and mission-critical systems are kept stable; new capabilities are built [[Headless Architecture|headless]] with modern frameworks.
- Safe internal tools prevent shadow [[Agentic AI|AI]] and channel entrepreneurial energy into sanctioned environments.
