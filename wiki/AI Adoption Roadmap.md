## Summary
An [[AI Adoption Roadmap]] outlines the stages and requirements for integrating [[Artificial Intelligence Integration|artificial intelligence]] into organizational operations.

## Key Points
- The roadmap includes establishing vocabulary, identifying [[AI Use Cases]], and defining target outcomes.
- It requires building organizational capacities and systems to support [[Agentic AI|AI]].
- [[Boxer]] is already progressing through its [[AI Adoption Roadmap]], focusing on advanced analytics and system integration.
