## Summary
Collaboration quotient (CQ) is a measure of an organization's ability to collaborate effectively, considered crucial for successful [[AI Implementation Roadmap|AI implementation]] and [[Enterprise Transformation|enterprise transformation]].

## Key Points
- CQ is analogous to IQ and EQ but focuses on collaborative capacity.
- High CQ is necessary for executing collaborative programs and [[Enterprise Transformation|transformation]] roadmaps.
- Organizations lacking CQ may opt for [[Principled Incrementalism|incrementalism]] or follow others.
