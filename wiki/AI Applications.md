## Summary
[[AI Applications]] refer to the use of [[Agentic AI|artificial intelligence]] technologies to automate and enhance various business functions, including content generation, [[Document Processing]], operations, and customer service.

## Key Points
- Encompasses a broad range of tools for automating tasks in marketing, construction, [[Human Resources|HR]], legal, leasing, and more.
- Includes specialized agents and features for summarization, [[AI Recommendations|recommendations]], and workflow automation.
- Supports multilingual summaries and case note automation.
