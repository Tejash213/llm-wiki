## Summary
Explains how early preservation of data transforms seemingly low-value information into strategic assets, illustrated by the lobster analogy.

## Key Points
- Data once considered waste can gain value over time as contexts and capabilities (analytics, AI) change.
- Early investment in data preservation enables competitive differentiation and new product possibilities.
- Storytelling (e.g., lobster as former waste turned delicacy) helps communicate the strategic importance of data retention.
