## Summary
Identifies dangers that arise when organizations allow rapid, decentralized creation of applications using AI, including fragmentation, governance failures, and performative initiatives.

## Key Points
- Rapid departmental app creation can institutionalize balkanization, producing ungoverned and unconnected applications.
- Ease of creation amplifies architectural and integration problems rather than solving them.
- Executives may pursue performative AI projects to preserve roles, delaying substantive transformation and increasing long-term risk.
