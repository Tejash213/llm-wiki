## Summary
The Pando metaphor compares a Self-Driving Company to a clonal stand of aspens connected by a single root system, illustrating a single organism with many visible parts that are interconnected and interdependent.

## Key Points
- Emphasizes that an organization is an interconnected network rather than a collection of independent parts.
- Highlights the need for a common root (integrated systems and data) that supports many visible functions (agents, teams, offices).
- Reinforces strategy to design for interdependence and systemic coherence.
