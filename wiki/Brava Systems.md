## Summary
Brava Systems is the rebranded company and product that succeeded Stemmons Enterprise, incorporating enhanced AI capabilities and representing the mature commercial platform derived from internal Boxer Property development.

## Key Points
- The rebrand to Brava Systems coincided with the introduction of enhanced AI functionality into the commercial product.
- Brava Systems is positioned as a platform for creating, managing, and deploying AI capabilities within enterprise operations.
- Boxer Property remained the first customer and design partner through the product evolution to Brava Systems.
