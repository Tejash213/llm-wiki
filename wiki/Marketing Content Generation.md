## Summary
Marketing Content Generation uses [[AI Agents]] to create, review, and suggest marketing materials and actions based on case context.

## Key Points
- Includes Marketing Standards Suggestion, Relay Content Agent, Brava Content Agent, and Marketing Self-Help.
- Automates content creation and provides recommendations for marketing cases.
- Utilizes trained agents to generate tailored marketing content.
