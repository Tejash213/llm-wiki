## Summary
[[Artificial Intelligence Integration]] refers to the strategic adoption of [[Agentic AI|AI]] technologies into the company's [[Unified Data and Systems]] infrastructure to enhance [[AI for Operational Efficiency|operational efficiency]] and decision-making.

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is positioned as a core pillar alongside [[Unified Data and Systems|unified data]] and [[Integrated Global Team|integrated global teams]].
- Integration focuses on combining automated workflows, advanced prompts, and [[Multi-Agent Workflows|multi-agent models]].
- The approach emphasizes [[Data Security|data security]], [[Transparency|transparency]], and scalable adoption.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] enables rapid growth in [[AI Use Cases|use cases]] and supports [[Safe Experimentation|safe experimentation]] and continuous learning.
