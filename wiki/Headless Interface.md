## Summary
A [[Headless Interface]] is a centralized logic, context, and [[Orchestration Layer]] that bridges company data, business logic, and external [[Platform-as-a-Service Providers|AI Providers]], resilient to changes in [[AI Model]]s and business structure.

## Key Points
- Functions as an intermediary between enterprise data, business logic, and [[AI Solutions Inventory]].
- Supports [[AI Integration]] and [[AI Orchestration]] without reliance on specific front-end applications.
- Enables resilience to model churn and business process changes.
