## Summary
[[Relay & Boxer]] are core enterprise platforms that house entities such as Position, Job, and Company, and integrate advanced search and [[AI Agents]] functionalities.

## Key Points
- Serve as interfaces for interacting with organizational data and [[AI agents]].
- Evolve from simple chat interfaces to command centers for orchestrating [[AI capabilities]].
- Integrate [[Vectorized Semantic Search]] and support [[Domain-Specific AI Agents]].
