## Summary
AI experience refers to the ability of [[Artificial Intelligence Integration]] to replicate and enhance human [[Knowledge Recall]] and [[Pattern Recognition]], which are traditionally considered core components of professional experience.

## Key Points
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] can be loaded with knowledge and recall it instantly.
- AI can be trained to recognize verbal and situational patterns.
- AI enables affordable, fast, consistent, and scalable replication of experience.
- Integrating AI shifts hiring focus toward aptitude and personality traits.
