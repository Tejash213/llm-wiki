## Summary
[[Integrated AI Systems]] refer to artificial intelligence solutions embedded within core organizational workflows, leveraging structured data and minimizing isolated, manual processes.

## Key Points
- [[AI Agents]] should be called by core systems, using canonical data sources to reduce user friction and ensure data integrity.
- Outputs from AI agents must be fed back into central systems as structured, durable assets.
- [[AI Integration]] supports abstraction, reusability, and enhances enterprise intelligence.
