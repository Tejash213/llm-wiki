## Summary
Transparency and control refer to maintaining detailed logs and oversight of [[Agentic AI|AI]] activity to ensure safe, monitored, and manageable [[Agentic AI|AI]] use within the company.

## Key Points
- Detailed logs are kept for all [[Agentic AI|AI]] activity.
- Monitoring prevents uncontrolled or unsafe [[Agentic AI|AI]] use.
- Supports safe experimentation and learning.
