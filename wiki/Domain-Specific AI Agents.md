## Summary
Domain-Specific [[AI Agents]] are specialized [[AI Model]]s provided with targeted knowledge sources to deliver expert-level responses in particular domains.

## Key Points
- Each agent is connected to distinct knowledge bases for relevant, permission-aware responses.
- Examples include marketing agents or client-specific agents.
- Envisioned to handle automated tasks and assessments with minimal human intervention.
