## Summary
Managers are positioned as [[Manager as AI Product Designer]], responsible for specifying behavior, supervising outputs, and orchestrating incremental rollout of [[AI Adoption]] within their domains.

## Key Points
- Managers own process outcomes, curate prompts, set guardrails, and monitor results.
- They bridge policy and practice, using AI-generated comments to inform policy changes.
- Upskilling is required to “train the intern” and “train the dragon,” transforming [[AI Capabilities]] from mystery to craft.
- Managers collaborate to refine prompts, taxonomy, and thresholds.
