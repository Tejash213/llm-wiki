## Summary
[[AI Integration]] refers to the process of embedding [[AI Capabilities]] into organizational workflows, systems, and applications, as exemplified by [[Brava Systems]].

## Key Points
- Brava enables seamless integration of [[Agentic AI|AI]] through configurable connectors and prompts.
- Supports multiple activation methods: user-driven, automated, and document-based [[AI Triggers]].
- Allows selection of standard, specialized, or custom [[AI Model]]s for tailored solutions.
