## Summary
[[AI Hiring]] is the practice of shifting recruitment focus from experience to aptitude and personality traits, leveraging [[Agentic AI|AI]] for [[Knowledge Recall]] and [[Pattern Recognition]].

## Key Points
- Traditional hiring emphasizes experience, aptitude, and personality.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] reduces the need for experience, enabling hiring of candidates with high aptitude and personality.
- [[Boxer]] uses [[Standards]] and [[Quest Forms]] to hire for aptitude rather than experience.
- [[AI Integration]] requires planning and changes to hiring processes.
