## Summary
Event-triggered agents are automations that execute based on data changes, inbound documents, or process milestones, reducing reliance on manual prompts.

## Key Points
- Run automatically in response to defined [[AI Triggers]] and conditions.
- Enhance workflow efficiency and consistency.
- Minimize manual intervention and prompt-driven usage.
