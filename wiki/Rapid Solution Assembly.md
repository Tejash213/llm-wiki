## Summary
Rapid solution assembly refers to the quick building and deployment of applications and [[Multi-Agent Workflows|AI Workflows]] using low/no-code and [[Agentic Framework|Agent Frameworks]], enabled by a shared data and [[Orchestration Layer]].

## Key Points
- Supports fast creation and deployment of chat interfaces, workflow integrations, and [[Agentic Flows]].
- Differentiator is coordinated, governed integration rather than speed alone.
- Reduces reliance on bespoke rewrites and siloed [[Point Solutions]].
