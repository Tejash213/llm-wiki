## Summary
HR stewardship encompasses both tactical and strategic mandates, including benefits, compliance, and shaping how the company integrates [[AI Integration]], sets expectations, and designs roles.

## Key Points
- HR is responsible for education, work design, policy and norm-setting, and performance metrics.
- HR can audit [[Job Descriptions]], automate repetitive tasks, and champion [[AI Literacy]].
- HR and IT jointly steward [[Socio-Technical Alignment]] and safety.
- HR leads programs such as [[Case-Type Discipline]] mastery, AI etiquette, audit automation, and override governance.
