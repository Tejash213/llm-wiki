## Summary
Human-centered [[AI Governance]] emphasizes designing and managing [[Agentic AI|AI]] systems so that data, technology, and people interact in accountable, informed workflows, prioritizing human judgment and oversight.

## Key Points
- Technology, data, [[Agentic AI|AI]], and people should work together in disciplined workflows.
- Companies must define norms for how [[AI Recommendations]] are surfaced, interpreted, and acted upon.
- Ignoring [[Agentic AI|AI]] is a governance failure; blind obedience is a safety failure.
- [[AI Recommendations]] should be treated as designed interventions from named architects, not anonymous surveillance.
- [[HR Stewardship]] and IT share stewardship: [[HR Stewardship]] for socio-technical adoption and work design; IT for tooling, architecture, and safety.
