## Summary
A knowledge base is an intelligent, searchable repository constructed by applying consistent metadata to content and linking it to structured operational data.

## Key Points
- Transforming document repositories into knowledge bases requires API connectivity and consistent metadata derived from systems of record.
- Knowledge bases enable agents and models to retrieve contextual information and support automated workflows and decision-making.
- They are a core outcome of data mastery and content understanding efforts.
