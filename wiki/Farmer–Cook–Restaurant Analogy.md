## Summary
The [[Farmer–Cook–Restaurant Analogy]] describes three organizational approaches to [[Agentic AI|AI]]: building everything (farmer), adapting and assembling (cook), or buying everything (restaurant).

## Key Points
- "Farmer": Full-stack building of platforms and APIs; suited for software firms or specialized needs.
- "Cook": Internal capability to assemble and adapt solutions within a [[Shared Kitchen]]; optimal for most enterprises.
- "Restaurant": Purely buying off-the-shelf products; fast but inflexible and prone to vendor lock-in.
- The analogy guides organizations to prioritize capability and adaptation over ubiquity or vendor dependence.
