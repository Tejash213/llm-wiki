## Summary
[[Human Intelligence (HI)]] refers to the cognitive abilities and judgment of people, which remain crucial for communication, decision-making, and validation in [[Multi-Agent Workflows|AI-augmented workflows]].

## Key Points
- [[Human Intelligence (HI)]] provides the decision and validation layers after AI processes are executed.
- [[Human Intelligence (HI)]] is the gold standard for communication but less relevant for routine data processing and research.
- The balance between [[Human Intelligence (HI)]] and [[Agentic AI|AI]] continues to evolve, with humans retaining important roles.
