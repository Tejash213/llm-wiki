## Summary
[[LLM Wikis]] are context layers that index source code, APIs, and documentation to make [[Coding Agents]] effective and controllable within organizations.

## Key Points
- Provide a structured knowledge layer for [[Agentic AI]] development.
- Essential for reliable change management and adaptation.
- Reduce the cost and time of agent development.
