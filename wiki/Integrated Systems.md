## Summary
[[Integrated Systems]] refer to the principle of treating agents, [[AI Orchestration]], domain expertise, and [[Global Talent]] as interconnected components of a unified, living organizational network.

## Key Points
- Capabilities are not separate features but parts of one interdependent system.
- The metaphor shifts from driving to Pando, a clonal stand of aspens connected by a single root system.
- The self-driving company operates as a unified organism, not a collection of disparate parts.
