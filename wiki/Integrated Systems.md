## Summary
Integrated Systems is the principle of treating agents, orchestration, domain expertise, and global talent as interconnected components of a unified operational organism rather than isolated features.

## Key Points
- Emphasizes system-level thinking where components are interdependent and reinforce each other.
- Requires consistent data models, semantic layers, and integration patterns to enable agent interactions.
- Contrasts with piecemeal feature additions that do not interoperate effectively.
