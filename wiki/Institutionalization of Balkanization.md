## Summary
A phenomenon where decentralized application creation leads to a persistent landscape of isolated, ungoverned applications across an organization.

## Key Points
- Departmental autonomy in app creation often produces disconnected systems and duplicated functionality.
- Balkanization increases complexity for debugging, management, security, and data integrity.
- Without central governance and integration strategies, the trend becomes institutionalized and costly to remediate.
