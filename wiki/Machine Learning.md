## Summary
Machine learning is presented as the family of analytical and predictive techniques that learn patterns from data and produce models for prediction and classification.

## Key Points
- Machine learning underpins predictive analytics and forms the basis for some aspects of LLM behavior.
- It is primarily discussed in the document as pattern recognition trained with historical data.
- Use cases in real estate include forecasting, predictive maintenance, and other operational predictions.
