## Summary
Principled incrementalism is an approach to organizational change where decisions are made incrementally, guided by a set of principles, rather than pursuing full-scale transformation.

## Key Points
- It is an alternative to collaborative transformational [[AI Roadmap]]s.
- Each decision is informed by organizational principles.
- Suitable for organizations not ready for high collaboration or rapid change.
