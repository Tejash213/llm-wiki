## Summary
[[Microsoft Azure AI Foundry]] is a recommended [[Platform-as-a-Service Providers]] provider for deploying [[Agentic AI|AI]] tools within [[Brava Systems]].

## Key Points
- Brava typically recommends [[Azure AI Foundry]] for [[Agentic AI|AI]] account setup.
- Supports [[AI Integration]] with Brava for secure and scalable [[AI Deployment Pipeline]].
- Enables configuration and management of [[AI Solutions Layer]].
