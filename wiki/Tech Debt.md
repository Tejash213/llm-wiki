## Summary
Technical debt refers to accumulated shortcuts, legacy systems, and architectural deficiencies that hinder future development and competitive agility.

## Key Points
- Tech debt slows innovation and increases the cost of implementing new capabilities, including AI-driven transformations.
- Companies burdened with tech debt are at risk of being outcompeted by AI-native entrants built on modern architectures.
- Addressing tech debt requires strategic investment in architecture, data models, and integration efforts.
