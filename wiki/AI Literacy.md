## Summary
[[AI Literacy]] involves educating employees on how to read, respond to, and challenge [[Agentic AI|AI]] prompts, emphasizing safety, etiquette, and the role of [[Agentic AI|AI]] as an assistive system.

## Key Points
- Education precedes enforcement in [[AI Adoption]].
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] is framed as a friend and assistive system, not an enemy or surveillance tool.
- Training includes how to engage with [[Agentic AI|AI]], codify dissent, and create feedback loops.
- [[HR Stewardship|HR]] leads [[AI Literacy]] and etiquette programs.
