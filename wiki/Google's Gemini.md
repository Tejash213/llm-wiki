## Summary
Google's Gemini is mentioned as an example of an AI provider that the orchestration layer can connect to, enabling selection of models from multiple vendors.

## Key Points
- Represents one of several commercial AI models accessible via the orchestration layer.
- Inclusion supports a model-agnostic routing approach for task-specific model selection.
- Part of the vendor-diverse ecosystem alongside providers like Anthropic, AWS, and Microsoft.
