## Summary
The [[Insight Agent]] translates broad human questions into explicit inquiry frames and data requirements, generating [[Pseudocode Interface|pseudocode]] as an intermediate representation for downstream processing.

## Key Points
- Recognizes domain terminology, expands synonyms, and maps business concepts to generic data elements.
- Generates pseudocode that is portable and reduces brittleness from schema drift.
- Canonicalizes terminology, favoring semantic terms over brittle field names.
- Acts as a durable interface between human intent and machine execution.
