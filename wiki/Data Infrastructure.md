## Summary
Data infrastructure refers to the centralized, normalized, and stewarded data systems that serve as the foundation for [[AI Integration]] and operational excellence.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer Property]] invested in building a strong data infrastructure over many years.
- [[Data Readiness]] is critical for unlocking [[Agentic AI|AI]]’s potential and supporting operational scalability.
- Data stewardship and singular normalized data are core principles at Boxer Property.
- Emerging [[Flexible AI Tools|AI Tools]] are beginning to automate data cleanup and [[Data Normalization]].
