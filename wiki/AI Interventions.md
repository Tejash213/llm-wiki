## Summary
[[AI Applications]] refer to the various types of [[AI Applications|artificial intelligence applications]] deployed within an organization to automate tasks, understand content, and enable advanced workflows.

## Key Points
- Includes data and workflow automation, content understanding, language processing, vision and speech, predictive analytics, generative models, and [[agentic systems]].
- Practical starting points are summarizing, looking up, and comparing information.
- Advanced interventions include structuring unstructured data and complex workflows.
