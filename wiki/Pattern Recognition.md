## Summary
Pattern recognition is the process by which individuals or [[Integrated AI Systems|AI systems]] assess situations, consider various factors, and determine outcomes based on observed patterns.

## Key Points
- Traditionally a key factor in evaluating professional experience.
- AI can be trained to recognize both verbal and situational patterns.
- Pattern recognition forms part of the value of human and [[AI Experience|AI-driven experience]].
