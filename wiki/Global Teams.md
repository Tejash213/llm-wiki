## Summary
Global teams refer to distributed workforce models that leverage talent from multiple regions, increasing organizational capacity for [[Agentic AI|AI]] and technology investment.

## Key Points
- Opening overseas offices enables incremental hiring and cost savings.
- [[Global Teams]] allow reinvestment in data and tech resources.
- [[Relay]] is an example of a company formed from such expansion.
