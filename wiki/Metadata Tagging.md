## Summary
Metadata tagging is the practice of applying consistent, rich metadata to unstructured content to enable intelligent search, discovery, and automated processing.

## Key Points
- Metadata often derives from structured core systems (e.g., property codes, tenant names, vendor lists) to link documents to operational entities.
- Proper tagging converts simple repositories into searchable knowledge bases that feed AI agents and workflows.
- Effective metadata tagging requires API connectivity to enterprise content management systems and integration with systems of record.
