## Summary
Vectorized Semantic Search is an advanced search capability that indexes large volumes of information and retrieves answers based on semantic meaning rather than keywords.

## Key Points
- Enables near-instantaneous retrieval of information (e.g., 500 milliseconds for seven million items).
- Supports complex queries and synthesized answers from indexed conversations.
- Demonstrated in the "[[Boxer]]" platform.
