## Summary
A progression describing how vehicles evolved from basic machines providing feedback to highly interconnected, self-aware systems, used as an analogy for broader technological and organizational change.

## Key Points
- Vehicle evolution moved from simple feedback mechanisms to safety and comfort features, then to interconnected systems and environmental awareness.
- The progression culminates in self-driving capabilities, representing increased system autonomy and changed human-vehicle interactions.
- The car metaphor is used to illustrate analogous transitions in companies and other systems toward greater automation and reduced reliance on human expertise.
