## Summary
[[AI Capabilities]] are foundational functions such as extraction, profiling, summarization, and comparison, which can be deployed within a governed architecture to support innovation across an organization.

## Key Points
- Capabilities include extraction, profiling, summarization, and comparison.
- Providing these capabilities within a safe, [[AI Governance|governed architecture]] empowers users to innovate responsibly.
- The roster of capabilities can be expanded over time, enabling organic emergence of [[AI Use Cases|use cases]].
