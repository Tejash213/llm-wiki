## Summary
The Sentient Enterprise describes a future organizational model where intelligent [[AI Orchestration Layer]]s are integral to every function, enabling proactive, data-driven operations.

## Key Points
- Moves beyond traditional, siloed enterprise models to a unified, intelligent system.
- Employs invisible [[AI Orchestration Layer]]s to automate and optimize workflows.
- Empowers strategic foresight through [[Agentic AI]]-driven analysis and action.
