## Summary
A configurable application platform allows enterprises to build and deploy business applications rapidly on top of a shared [[Data Layer]], minimizing reliance on monolithic or [[Point Solutions]].

## Key Points
- [[Boxer]] replaced large commercial platforms with an internal configurable layer.
- Applications are built via [[Low-Code - No-Code Platform|Low-Code/No-Code Platform]] on shared data, reducing bespoke rewrites.
- The platform supports [[Rapid Solution Assembly]] and integration with [[AI Orchestration]].
- Many commercial apps are seen as wrappers around basic functions.
