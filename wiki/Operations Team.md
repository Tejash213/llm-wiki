## Summary
The operations team at Boxer is mentioned as an early internal user of AI features, primarily to generate more effective narratives for customer communication and to support action generation from customer requests.

## Key Points
- Operations has been using AI to improve communication narratives sent to customers.
- The team uses AI to help convert customer requests into actionable items.
- Operations is an example of a functional team where AI augments routine knowledge and pattern-recognition tasks.
