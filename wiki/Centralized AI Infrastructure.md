## Summary
Centralized AI Infrastructure is a shared services model for managing core [[AI Capabilities]], embeddings, and data services, reducing redundancy and organizational risk.

## Key Points
- Eliminates inefficiencies of decentralized, bespoke [[AI Solutions Layer]] systems.
- Provides managed, secure foundations for governed applications.
- Enables cost-effective scaling of [[Automated AI Triggers|AI activations]] across the enterprise.
