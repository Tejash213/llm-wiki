## Summary
[[AI Solutions Inventory]] refers to the collection of [[Agentic AI|AI]] tools, [[Agentic Systems]], and workflows deployed across [[Boxer]], including chatbots, workflow triggers, and purpose-built agents.

## Key Points
- Includes central chat bots, case buttons, workflow triggers, and domain-specific agents.
- Utilizes multiple providers and models: [[Microsoft Azure AI Foundry|Azure OpenAI]] (GPT-4o), Google [[Gemini]], in-house GPT-4i nano, and [[Foundry]]-based agents.
- Enables rapid creation and deployment of new [[AI Solutions Inventory]] from the UI.
