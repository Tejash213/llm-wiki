## Summary
Tools and capacities refer to the technical and organizational capabilities required to implement AI effectively, including data infrastructure, workflow systems, and AI models.

## Key Points
- Effective AI adoption depends on data readiness, workflow automation, and integration of AI capabilities like content understanding and predictive models.
- Organizational capacities include hiring practices, training, and cultural readiness to use AI-enhanced tools.
- Tools may include transcription systems, vision/speech intelligence platforms, LLMs, and analytics pipelines.
