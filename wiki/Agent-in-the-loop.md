## Summary
Agent-in-the-loop refers to processes where specialized agents execute tasks or workflows autonomously or semi-autonomously, often under human-defined policies and oversight.

## Key Points
- Specialized agents perform operational tasks when confidence, policy, and integration permit automated execution.
- Can be combined with human oversight for exceptions or high-impact decisions.
- Requires robust integration points and clear policies to maintain control and auditability.
