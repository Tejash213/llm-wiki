## Summary
[[Data Normalization]] is the process of structuring and standardizing organizational data to create a unified source of truth, a foundational capability of [[Brava Systems]] and [[STEMIs Enterprise]].

## Key Points
- Enables organizations to work with a single, unified dataset.
- Supports interoperability and [[Data Integration]] of applications.
- Facilitates reliable [[AI Deployment Pipeline]] and [[AI Orchestration]].
