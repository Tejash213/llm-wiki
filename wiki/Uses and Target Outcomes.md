## Summary
Uses and target outcomes describe the practical applications and desired business results from AI investments, such as improved customer narratives, automated actions from requests, predictive analytics, and operational efficiencies.

## Key Points
- Specific uses include narrative generation for customer communication and converting requests into operational actions.
- Target outcomes emphasize efficiency gains, freeing human time for higher-value work, and improved decision-making via predictive insights.
- Outcomes require supportive tools: data systems, automation, and cultural/hiring adjustments.
