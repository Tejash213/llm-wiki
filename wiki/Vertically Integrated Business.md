## Summary
A vertically integrated business performs multiple stages of its value chain internally. The document describes Boxer as vertically integrated within real estate, handling many functions and requiring coordination across teams.

## Key Points
- Vertical integration at Boxer means a broad scope of internal functions (operations, construction, property management, etc.) requiring coordination.
- Such businesses can benefit from AI to streamline cross-functional workflows and centralize knowledge.
- Vertical integration increases the need for systems, data infrastructure, and workflow automation to coordinate activities efficiently.
