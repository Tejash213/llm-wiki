## Summary
The [[General - Coordinator Agent|General/Coordinator Agent]] manages [[AI Orchestration]], delegation, resource usage, and policy enforcement among agents, ensuring efficient teamwork and cost control.

## Key Points
- Delegates tasks, enforces role boundaries, and arbitrates resource usage.
- Implements planning, chunking, retries, and error handling.
- Provides a unified entry point for broad [[Artificial General Intelligence|AGI]]-like behaviors.
- Maintains logs, budget/time caps, and kill switches.
