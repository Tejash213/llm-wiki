[[Leads and Leasing Operations]] AI tools automate lease writing, lead identification, quoting reviews, and renewal/new lease outlines.

## Key Points
- Features Lease Writing Agent, Find Similar Leads, Renewal/New Lease Writing Outline, and Lead Quoting Review.
- Uses vector data stores to find similar leads.
- Organizes information for lease renewals and new leases.
