## Summary
Organizational technology efforts should prioritize robust architecture and infrastructure (the foundation) rather than focusing first on flashy features that may not scale or endure.

## Key Points
- Shiny, short-term features often fail when underlying architecture is weak; scalability and longevity require a strong technical foundation.
- Investing in infrastructure and systems enables future innovation and prevents costly rework as needs evolve.
- Organizations that prioritize foundational work can adapt faster and support advanced use cases like large-scale AI.
