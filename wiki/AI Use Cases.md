## Summary
[[AI Use Cases]] are specific applications of [[Agentic AI|artificial intelligence]] within business workflows, ranging from simple tasks to complex process automation.

## Key Points
- Common initial use cases include chat-driven policy lookup, email-to-task automation, document content extraction, and summarization.
- Use cases can be expanded and combined into dynamic processes as familiarity grows.
- Low-risk use cases are recommended for early adoption.
