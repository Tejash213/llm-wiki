## Summary
Boxer Property is the organization that internally developed a comprehensive enterprise platform for real estate operations and served as the first customer and design partner for the commercial product.

## Key Points
- Fifteen years of internal software development at Boxer Property led to a platform to manage its real estate operations, evolving with AI from 2018 onward.
- Boxer Property is cited as the first customer and enduring design partner for the externally available product.
- The internal needs of Boxer Property motivated the integration focus on operational flexibility distinct from accounting systems.
