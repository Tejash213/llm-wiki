## Summary
Boxer Property is a vertically integrated [[Commercial Real Estate]] company that has leveraged [[Agentic AI|AI]] and [[Data Infrastructure]] to drive [[AI for Operational Efficiency|Operational Efficiency]], [[Employee Fulfillment]], and [[Innovation Platform|innovation]].

## Key Points
- Founded in 1992, Boxer Property specializes in office spaces but also manages retail, hotels, land, restaurants, and golf courses.
- The company adopted a "full stack" approach, handling many operations internally.
- Boxer Property invested heavily in normalized, centralized [[Data Infrastructure]] as a foundation for [[AI Integration]].
- Utilized [[Brava Systems]] to implement a balanced, incremental [[Incremental AI Rollout|AI rollout]] across business functions.
- Established remote offices globally to support [[Remote Knowledge Work|knowledge work]] and enable 24/7 operations.
- Promotes a culture of [[Healthy Risk Taking|healthy risk-taking]], experimentation, and learning with [[Flexible AI Tools|AI tools]].
