## Summary
The [[AI Solution Repository]] is a back-end list of [[Multi-Agent Workflows|AI workflows]] and [[AI Agents|agents]], detailing their [[AI Model|models]], runtimes, [[AI Triggers|triggers]], and functions within enterprise processes.

## Key Points
- Repository includes workflows for [[Job Descriptions|job descriptions]], property takeover checklists, deal pipeline strategy match, report engineering, and [[Document Processing|document intelligence]].
- Each entry specifies model/runtime (e.g., [[Microsoft Azure AI Foundry|Azure OpenAI GPT-5 Mini]], [[Gemini]], [[Microsoft Foundry]] agents) and trigger type.
- Supports both single-shot prompts and complex [[Agentic Flows|agentic flows]].
