## Summary
Hiring for aptitude is a strategic hiring approach recommended in the document where organizations prioritize flexibility, problem solving, communication skills, and adaptability over decades of specific experience because AI can handle many knowledge-recall tasks.

## Key Points
- The document suggests shifting compensation and hiring focus from experience (traditionally ~75%) toward personality and aptitude (~25% or more) as AI handles knowledge tasks.
- Candidates with problem-solving ability, communication skills, and system literacy are preferred in an AI-augmented workplace.
- This approach reduces dependence on legacy manual processes and enables leveraging AI to scale knowledge work.
