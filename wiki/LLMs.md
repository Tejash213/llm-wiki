## Summary
[[LLMs]] ([[Large Language Models]]) are generative AI models used by [[Boxer]] for tasks such as summarization, content understanding, and workflow automation.

## Key Points
- [[Boxer]] utilizes [[Microsoft Azure AI Foundry]] (GPT-4o), Google [[Gemini]], and in-house GPT-4i nano models.
- [[LLMs]] are integrated into chatbots, workflow triggers, and advanced agents.
- Understanding context windows and prompt engineering is essential for effective use.
