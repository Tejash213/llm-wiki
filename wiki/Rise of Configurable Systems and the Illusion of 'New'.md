## Summary
Explains how low-code/no-code and AI-assisted application creation are iterations of earlier trends in configurable systems, and that integration remains the core challenge.

## Key Points
- Configurable platforms reduced the need for custom development by enabling rapid assembly of applications.
- Current AI-driven app creation is conceptually similar to these platforms and not entirely novel in addressing app assembly.
- The enduring challenge is integration: connecting applications to common data, governance, security, and interoperability frameworks.
