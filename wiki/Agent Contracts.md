## Summary
Agent Contracts are explicit definitions of agent roles, responsibilities, and payload handoff requirements within a [[Multi-Agent AI Orchestration]], ensuring clarity and disciplined delegation.

## Key Points
- [[Insight Agent]] frames intent and generates pseudocode.
- [[Understanding Agent]] resolves schema and semantics.
- [[Search Agent]] performs permissioned, scoped retrieval.
- [[Action Agent]] completes tasks and presents results.
- Delegation and payload handoff are mandatory; violations are logged and penalized.
