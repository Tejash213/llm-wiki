## Summary
Companies undergo an evolution analogous to technology: from basic computing adoption through networking and software development to internet-era operations and now AI integration.

## Key Points
- Historical stages include digital computers, information networking, software development, the Internet, and increasingly AI.
- These technological layers converge to change how companies operate and how work is structured.
- The transition affects the relationship between people, companies, and the nature of work and value creation.
