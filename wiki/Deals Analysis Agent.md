## Summary
The Deals Analysis Agent is a domain expert agent focused on evaluating investment or asset deals using a dedicated semantic layer, glossaries, due diligence documentation, and access to historical deal data.

## Key Points
- Maintains a semantic layer and glossary plus due diligence checklists and SOPs to standardize evaluation.
- Provides access to the corpus of historically evaluated deals to inform scoring and decisions.
- Works with helper subagents (profiling, scoring, thesis-matching) to streamline evaluation and align findings with investment theses.
