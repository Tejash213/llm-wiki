## Summary
The [[Deals Analysis Agent]] is a [[Domain Expert Agents|domain expert agent]] designed to streamline deal evaluation and alignment with investment theses using a dedicated semantic layer and helper subagents.

## Key Points
- Utilizes a semantic layer and glossary to interpret deal data.
- Supports due diligence with process documentation, checklists, and SOPs.
- Accesses all historical deals for comprehensive analysis.
- Helper subagents assist with profiling, scoring, and thesis-matching.
