## Summary
The Wiki Generation Skill is the prompt template used by the FutureSense AI pipeline to extract and structure knowledge from source documents into Obsidian-compatible Markdown wiki pages.

## Key Points
- Defines the role of the AI as a knowledge curator for FutureSense.
- Enforces a strict page template with frontmatter, Summary, Key Points, Related Concepts, and Source Documents.
- Requires [[LLM Wikis|Double Bracket]] Obsidian wikilinks to reference related concepts.
- Iteratively improved by the SkillOpt training loop to produce best_skill.md.
- Ensures comprehensive extraction of every distinct concept, entity, and theme.
