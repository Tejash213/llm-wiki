## Summary
STEMIs Enterprise is the original [[Low-Code - No-Code Platform|Low-Code/No-Code Platform]] operations platform that evolved into [[Brava Systems]], designed to centralize and normalize enterprise data for improved business operations.

## Key Points
- Provided tools for integrating data into a centralized repository or connecting with existing structures.
- Enabled organizations to build and maintain a normalized enterprise dataset.
- Supported creation of interoperable applications using configurable tools.
- Offered a universal interface for overseeing operations and aggregating information from other tools.
- Streamlined business processes for clients such as [[Boxer Property]].
