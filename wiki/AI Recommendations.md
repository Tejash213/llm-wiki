## Summary
[[AI Recommendations]] are advisory outputs from [[Integrated AI Systems|AI Systems]] that prompt users to take action, reclassify cases, or engage with workflows, requiring human accountability and oversight.

## Key Points
- [[AI Recommendations]] should not be ignored; users must engage, comply, or dissent with reasons.
- AI advice is authored by identifiable architects and should be treated as designed policy instrumentation.
- Recommendations are visible, attributable, and auditable.
- AI is fallible and requires organizational scoring, monitoring, and iterative improvement.
