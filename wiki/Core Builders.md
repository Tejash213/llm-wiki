## Summary
Core builders are a small group of [[AI Solution Architecture]] architects, process engineers, agent writers, and [[Data Integration]] specialists responsible for delivering systemic [[AI Strategy|AI impact]] within organizations.

## Key Points
- Typically 10–20 people in a 1,000-person firm.
- Design, assemble, and govern [[AI Solutions Inventory]] using shared infrastructure.
- Central to the [[SEAL Team]] and the "cook" approach.
