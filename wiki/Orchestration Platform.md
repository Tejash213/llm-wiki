## Summary
An [[Orchestration Platform]] is a centralized system for integrating and managing multiple [[AI Applications]], enabling communication, trust, and flexibility across an enterprise.

## Key Points
- [[Orchestration Platform]]s prevent siloed [[AI Implementation Roadmap]]s and data fragmentation.
- They provide common tools and interfaces, similar to the connectivity of smartphone applications.
- [[Brava Systems]] developed such a platform for enterprise AI.
