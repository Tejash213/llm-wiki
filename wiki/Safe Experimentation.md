## Summary
Safe experimentation refers to encouraging employees to explore [[AI Capabilities]] within approved platforms and controlled environments, supporting learning and innovation.

## Key Points
- Experimentation is encouraged within enterprise platforms, not public tools.
- Employees are urged to capture ideas via ad hoc cases or suggestion entities.
- Learning and understanding [[AI Capabilities]] are key to meaningful change.
