## Summary
A strong [[Technological Foundation]], including robust architecture and infrastructure, is critical for scaling and sustaining advanced features and innovations.

## Key Points
- Investing in foundational technology is more important than focusing on flashy features.
- Businesses with strong foundations can adapt and innovate more effectively.
- Features built on weak infrastructure will not scale or last.
