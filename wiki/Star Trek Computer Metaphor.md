## Summary
A communicative metaphor that likens future AGI capabilities to the all-knowing, conversational computer from Star Trek to make abstract technical futures tangible for stakeholders.

## Key Points
- Metaphors like the Star Trek computer simplify complex ideas and generate excitement among non-technical audiences.
- The metaphor is useful for explaining the practical steps needed to approach industry-specific AGI, such as infrastructure and system integration.
- Clear metaphors can align stakeholders around a vision while helping define actionable milestones toward that vision.
