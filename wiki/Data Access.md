## Summary
[[Data Access]] encompasses the methods and types of data provided to [[AI Model]]s, including [[User-Provided Data]], contextual, and solution-integrated data.

## Key Points
- Data can be provided directly by users, derived from [[User-Context Data]], or integrated from corporate systems.
- [[Data Access]] is essential for AI accuracy and relevance.
- Options for [[Data Access]] vary based on [[AI Solution Architecture]].
