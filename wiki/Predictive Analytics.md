## Summary
Predictive analytics is one of the integrated AI capabilities used to analyze patterns and forecast outcomes within enterprise operations.

## Key Points
- Predictive models inform proactive workflows by identifying likely future events or risks (e.g., lease expirations, construction delays).
- When combined with metadata and agentic flows, predictions can trigger tasks, alerts, or automated remediation.
- The orchestration layer mediates the use of predictive outputs to ensure they feed into appropriate operational processes.
