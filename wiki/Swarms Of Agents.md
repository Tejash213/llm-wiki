## Summary
The anticipated evolution of agentic systems into coordinated groups or swarms of agents that collaborate to solve complex problems with increasing autonomy.

## Key Points
- Represents a progression from single-prompt interactions to multi-agent collaboration.
- Enables tackling complex, multi-step problems by distributing work across specialized agents.
- Relies on orchestration, shared schemas, and governance to ensure coherent outcomes.
