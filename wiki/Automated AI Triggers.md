## Summary
Automated AI triggers are mechanisms that activate [[Agentic AI|AI]] functions based on scheduled events or the arrival of new information, enabling systemic, automatic deployment of [[Agentic AI|AI]] within organizational workflows.

## Key Points
- Automated activation captures more value than human-initiated [[Agentic AI|AI]] tool usage.
- Scheduled events (e.g., monthly reports) and event-driven triggers (e.g., arrival of [[Invoices]], sales leads) are natural activation points.
- [[AI Agents]] can analyze, summarize, and augment information automatically, improving workflow outputs without requiring user initiation.
- This approach delivers benefits systemically, similar to adding fluoride to the water supply.
