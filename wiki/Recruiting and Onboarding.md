## Summary
[[Recruiting and Onboarding]] AI tools automate case summaries, resource requests, instructions, and follow-ups for new hires and candidates.

## Key Points
- Includes Fallback Scenario, I-9 Verification, New Hire Resource Requests, Self-Onboarding Instructions, and more.
- Provides automated case notes for background checks, application follow-ups, and onboarding timing.
- Supports assignment of training and job posting updates.
