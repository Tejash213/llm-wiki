## Summary
A theme emphasizing that system architecture, data models, and integration matter more for long-term value than individual AI models alone.

## Key Points
- Effective architectures and well-structured data layers are critical to realizing value from AI, beyond selecting models.
- Poor architecture leads to fragility, integration problems, and operational debt even with strong models.
- Sustainable AI adoption requires investment in data modeling, governance, and integration, not just model experimentation.
