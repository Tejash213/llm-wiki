## Summary
AI-in-the-loop design integrates [[Integrated AI Systems|AI Systems]] into workflows where human judgment is needed, favoring [[AI Recommendations]] and visibility over silent [[Automation]].

## Key Points
- [[AI Recommendations]] are preferred over silent [[Automation]] when judgment is required.
- [[AI Interventions]] should be visible, attributable, and auditable.
- Thresholds are established for [[Automation]] versus mandatory human review.
- Continuous improvement is enabled through feedback loops and [[AI Model]] retraining.
