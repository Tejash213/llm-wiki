## Summary
Governance metrics are measurable behaviors and outcomes used to track engagement with [[Agentic AI|AI]], [[Case-Type Discipline]] accuracy, [[AI Recommendations]] quality, and downstream impacts on reporting and risk.

## Key Points
- Metrics include view rate, response rate, override reasons, escalation outcomes, and compliance.
- [[Agentic AI|AI]] engagement and process adherence are incorporated into performance evaluations.
- Safety policies require reporting unsafe [[Agentic AI|AI]] prompts and never following recommendations that violate security or policy.
