## Summary
A high-level goal is a specific, measurable objective that supports the [[Mission Statement]], defining key areas of focus for [[Strategic AI Planning]].

## Key Points
- High-level goals include clarity on cost, results, and accountability; effective capabilities and tech stack; incremental enterprise-wide adoption; model and platform independence; and safety, security, and compliance.
- Each high-level goal is supported by [[Mid-Level Goal]]s and [[Task]]s.
