## Summary
Enterprise transformation is the process of fundamentally changing a company's operations, processes, and workforce to leverage new technologies, such as [[Agentic AI|AI]], for improved flexibility and value.

## Key Points
- Transformation goes beyond improving existing processes and seeks to redefine how work is done.
- The shift from experience-based to [[Aptitude-Based Hiring]] is a key component.
- Competing against native [[Agentic AI|AI]] companies and new talent is a central challenge.
- [[Flexible AI Tools]], tool-based approaches are preferred over siloed, vendor-driven solutions.
