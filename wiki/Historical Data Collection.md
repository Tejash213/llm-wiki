## Summary
The practice of preserving operational data over long periods, which can transform overlooked records into strategic assets for analytics and AI.

## Key Points
- The company began storing operational real estate data over 15 years ago, intentionally preserving material others discarded.
- Longitudinal datasets provide unique opportunities for trend analysis, model training, and knowledge encoding for AI agents.
- Early collection practices can convert low-value or discarded information into high-value strategic assets.
