## Summary
Vision and speech intelligence encompass AI technologies that analyze images, video, and audio to detect events, objects, activities, and spoken content for downstream use in operations and safety.

## Key Points
- Vision intelligence can perform tasks like video recognition to detect events (e.g., theft or other incidents) and extract visual insights.
- Speech intelligence involves transcribing spoken words and understanding the meaning or themes in real time.
- These capabilities enable monitoring, safety, content understanding, and automation in real estate operations.
