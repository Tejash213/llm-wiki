## Summary
Ontology in enterprise technology refers to the structured, well-documented schema and relationships within company data, forming the foundation for consistent logic and [[AI Deployment Pipeline]].

## Key Points
- A clear ontology enables integration, governance, and consistent access layers.
- Supports the [[Data-First Strategy]] and underpins configurable applications and [[AI Orchestration]].
- Essential for auditability, metadata management, and compliance.
