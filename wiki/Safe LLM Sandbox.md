## Summary
Safe LLM Sandbox is a secure environment for internal experimentation and prototyping with [[Large Language Models]], ensuring outputs are controlled and risk is minimized during early [[AI Deployment Pipeline]].

## Key Points
- Provides a controlled space for internal play and prototyping with LLMs.
- Ensures outputs are restricted and risk is minimized.
- Supports comment-only agents and incremental deployment.
