## Summary
[[Intelligent Enterprise Architecture]] refers to a composable, data-driven framework that integrates systems and [[Artificial Intelligence Integration]] into a unified ecosystem for organizational innovation and efficiency.

## Key Points
- Moves away from siloed, pre-configured software toward dynamic, composable frameworks.
- Built on a centralized [[Data Layer]], low-code/no-code application development, and [[AI Orchestration]].
- Enables rapid innovation and seamless integration of disparate systems.
