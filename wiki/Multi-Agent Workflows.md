## Summary
Multi-agent workflows involve the coordinated operation of multiple [[AI Agents]] to analyze, classify, and process documents or cases within the enterprise.

## Key Points
- Workflows analyze documents, classify them (contracts, loans, leases), and route them to specialized agents.
- Enables detailed processing and automation across departments.
- Supports scalable adoption and rapid growth in [[AI Use Cases]].
