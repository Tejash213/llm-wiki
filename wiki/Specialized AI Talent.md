## Summary
Specialized AI Talent refers to roles like data scientists and AI solution architects who can be sourced globally to design systems, data strategies, and agent implementations that may be difficult to hire domestically.

## Key Points
- Global hiring can make specialized AI roles more affordable and accessible compared to domestic markets.
- These specialists enable the design and integration of agent architectures, semantic layers, and orchestration systems.
- Presence of specialized talent accelerates organizational adoption of systems and tooling.
