## Summary
[[Strategic AI Planning]] is a framework for organizations to develop a clear, ambitious, and flexible [[AI Roadmap]] for AI-driven transformation, integrating human and artificial intelligence.

## Key Points
- The framework includes defining a [[Mission Statement]], [[High-Level Goal]], [[Mid-Level Goal]], and specific [[Task]]s.
- It emphasizes [[Transparency]], accountability, incremental adoption, model and platform independence, and safety, security, and compliance.
- [[Brava Systems]] provides tools and support to help organizations execute their strategic AI plans.
