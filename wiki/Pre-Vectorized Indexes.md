## Summary
Indexes created ahead of queries that store vectorized or digest representations of data to enable cost-efficient and fast AI retrieval operations.

## Key Points
- Serve as first-pass resources to answer routine queries with minimal token and compute usage.
- Can be combined with AI-generated digests created as data enters the system.
- Significantly reduce operational cost in high-usage environments when agents are designed to leverage them.
