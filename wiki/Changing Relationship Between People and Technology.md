## Summary
Describes how user expectations, skills, and interactions with technology have shifted as systems become more autonomous and diagnostically capable.

## Key Points
- Users increasingly rely on automated systems for tasks formerly requiring specialized skills, such as vehicle maintenance or navigation.
- Familiar assumptions about required human knowledge are disappearing as devices and services self-diagnose and self-manage.
- This shift affects organizational roles and individual value propositions within companies as automation replaces memory- and experience-based tasks.
