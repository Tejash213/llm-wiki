## Summary
Task or case management is the core systemic logic for tracking and coordinating work across domains (work orders, HR, legal), serving as a repeatable pattern for platform automation.

## Key Points
- Many operational functions can be implemented as task or case templates, enabling rapid deployment of similar solutions across domains.
- Agentic workflows often culminate in creating tasks or cases assigned to responsible individuals with embedded context and links.
- A central platform enables management, scaling, and governance of these tasks and cases across the enterprise.
