## Summary
[[OpenCLAW]] is referenced as a platform used for building bespoke [[AI Solutions Inventory]], which can contribute to fragmented and fragile ecosystems if not governed properly within organizations.

## Key Points
- [[OpenCLAW]] is an example of a platform for [[Citizen-Led AI Development]].
- Solutions built on [[OpenCLAW]] may be dependent on individual creators and lack sustainability.
- Use of such platforms can lead to brittle constructs that require replacement if not integrated into a governed architecture.
