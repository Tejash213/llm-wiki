## Summary
Principled innovation involves guiding [[AI Adoption]] with high-level principles rather than rigid roadmaps or tactical checklists.

## Key Points
- [[AI Roadmap]]s and checklists become obsolete as technology landscapes shift.
- Principles provide flexible guidance for decision-making.
- Businesses benefit from a playbook of adaptable, high-level principles.
