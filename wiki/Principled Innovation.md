## Summary
An approach that favors high-level, enduring principles to guide decision-making and innovation rather than rigid roadmaps or narrow tactical checklists that quickly become obsolete.

## Key Points
- Roadmaps can break when circumstances change; principles provide durable guidance across shifting contexts.
- A principles-based playbook focuses on how to approach decisions rather than prescribing fixed steps.
- This approach enables flexible, context-aware execution and better long-term alignment across teams.
