## Summary
Describes the historical shift from costly, bespoke software development to widespread adoption of SaaS products that standardized common business functions.

## Key Points
- Initially, software development was expensive and reserved for core systems; networking and object-oriented programming reduced barriers.
- The 2000–2015 era saw a rapid growth of specialized SaaS products focusing on tracking entities and providing user interfaces for specific departments.
- Market competition drove vendors to add many features to stand out, often leading to bloated applications and siloed data.
