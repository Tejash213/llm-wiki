## Summary
Summaries or digest representations of data created by AI as it enters the system to enable fast subsequent queries against compact representations.

## Key Points
- Created at ingestion time to provide efficient first-pass answers and reduce token usage.
- Often stored alongside or within pre-vectorized indexes for fast retrieval.
- Integral to cost governance strategies for token-based AI billing.
