## Summary
A document extraction tool identifies document types, routes them to specialized agents, and extracts structured data for use in datasets and workflows.

## Key Points
- The tool first classifies documents (e.g., loan agreement vs. lease) and then delegates to specialized extraction agents.
- Extracted data can be written to structured datasets or used to trigger further automated workflows and task generation.
- Integration with content repositories and metadata enables accurate routing and context-aware extraction.
