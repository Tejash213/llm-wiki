## Summary
A model for organizational AI ownership describing three complementary roles: a tech-business bridge leader, a committee of department leaders, and technical experts, all supported by CEO backing.

## Key Points
- The first vertex is a leader who understands both technology and business and can secure organizational buy-in.
- The second vertex is a committee of department leaders providing operational perspectives and cross-functional alignment.
- The third vertex consists of technical experts who design and implement systems; CEO sponsorship is required to provide authority and resources.
