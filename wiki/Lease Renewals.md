## Summary
Lease renewals are used as an example domain where agentic workflows can run periodic analyses, search external information about tenants, and create tasks with contextual links for asset managers.

## Key Points
- Agents can schedule monthly analyses to identify agreements approaching expiration and synthesize external news and internal lease clauses.
- The output is embedded directly into tasks assigned to relevant asset managers, with links to specific lease clauses.
- This pattern is representative of repeatable business templates suitable for automation across other contract lifecycle events.
