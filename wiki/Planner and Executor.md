## Summary
The Planner and Executor pattern pairs a planning agent that designs multi-step workflows with an execution agent that carries out tasks, coordinating across memory, iteration, and external systems.

## Key Points
- Planner handles breaking work into chunks, sequencing steps, and re-planning when conditions change.
- Executor performs the operational steps, invoking systems, APIs, or human tasks as required.
- Useful for larger use cases such as large-scale data quality reviews, document migrations, and multi-stage normalizations.
