## Summary
Shared kitchen refers to central services, curated data, and safe interfaces that enable internal teams to assemble and adapt [[AI Solutions Inventory]] efficiently.

## Key Points
- Provides ingredients (central services, curated data) for internal "cooks" to configure and adapt solutions.
- Reduces the need for bespoke supply chains and supports safe user customization.
- Forms the foundation for the "cook" approach in the [[Farmer–Cook–Restaurant Analogy]].
